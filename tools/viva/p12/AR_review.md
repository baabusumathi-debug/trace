# AR (Arrest) — fact-check review, 23 Nov 2026

Sources fetched: AHA 2025 Part 11 Post-Cardiac Arrest Care (cpr.heart.org, full text); AHA 2025 Adult ALS (cpr.heart.org); AHA 2025 Adult BLS (cpr.heart.org); AHA 2025 Part 10 Special Circumstances (cpr.heart.org, hyperthermia, hypothermia, pregnancy, AFE, asthma, hyperkalaemia); AHA 2025 newsroom release (choking, infants); RCUK/ERC 2025 ALS and post-resuscitation care summaries (resus.org.uk); RCUK 2021 post-resuscitation care; Cheskes 2011 abstract (PMID 21690495); Retraction Watch 29 Dec 2025 (BMJ retraction of the Taiwanese IO vs IV trial).

## Changes (where: old → new; source)
1. Packet open (P12-1001): "special arrests: pregnancy, asthma, poisoning and cold" → "pregnancy, asthma, heat and cold". The section teaches no poisoning; it does teach heat stroke.
2. Packet open: "Two grand-test misses… both from the later sections" → "one in the section on drugs and access, and one in the hours after ROSC". The IO miss is in section 3 and the temperature miss in section 5.
3. ACLS-48 lesson: added "The European guideline still gives half a milligram, to the same maximum, so in an American-style question half a milligram is the old answer." Takeaway now says "older American dose". Spine, drill and trap changed to match. Source: ERC/RCUK 2025 ALS (atropine 500 mcg, repeat to 3 mg).
4. ACLS-19 lesson: "The pooled result didn't change with or without it" (not verified) → "the two that stand tell the same story". "the odds of sustained ROSC were lower" → "in the pooled analysis the odds…". Source: AHA 2025 ALS (systematic review OR 0.89 for sustained ROSC with IO); Retraction Watch (the Taiwan trial was retracted).
5. PCA-49 lesson: "Both guidelines of 2025 say two to seven. The European guideline of 2021 said two to five" → "The American guideline of 2025 says two to seven. Older European guidance, from 2015, said two to five." The ERC 2025 and 2021 summaries give no MRI window; AHA 2025 says 2–7 days. The trap truth was changed to match.
6. ACLS-70 lesson: "Three are named" → "Three are the ones to know", and added AHA's fourth favourable sign: no restricted diffusion on MRI at 2–7 days. Source: AHA 2025 Part 11.
7. PCA-47 box: S-100B and GFAP "not recommended" → "value uncertain" (AHA 2025: other biomarkers COR 2b, usefulness uncertain). Lesson: "The European guideline doesn't recommend it yet" → "The European algorithm still uses only NSE…".
8. ACLS-61 lesson: added that the 32–37.5 °C range first appeared in the 2023 focused update. This matches the pack explanation; AHA 2025 joined it to the 36-hour minimum.

## Verified unchanged (selection)
- **Post-ROSC (AHA 2025):** SpO2 90–98%. This is correct: the task list's "92–98" is the 2020 figure, and the draft rightly calls it old. Also: MAP ≥65 with no SBP target; PaCO2 35–45; glucose: avoid <70 and >180; 32–37.5 °C, ≥36 h (2a); rewarm ≤0.5 °C/h; no prehospital cold IV fluid (3); no seizure prophylaxis; myoclonus without an EEG correlate not treated; emergent angiography for STE, and no emergent angiography for comatose patients without STE; head-to-pelvis CT and echo 2b; multimodal prognosis at ≥72 h after normothermia; NSE or NfL 2b; normal NSE, continuous EEG and withdrawal motor response are favourable; preserved pupil or corneal reflexes are not favourable; organ donation COR 1.
- **ERC 2025:** SpO2 94–98%; MAP >60–65; fever prevention ≤37.5 °C for 36–72 h (the 2021 wording was >37.7 °C for ≥72 h); the "two or more" rule with all six predictors; NSE >60 at 48 and/or 72 h; repeat CT at 72–96 h; levetiracetam or valproate.
- **ALS:** ETCO2 ≥10, ideally ≥20 (AHA text); ROSC shown by a sudden rise >10 mmHg, with no 40; no adult diastolic target (AHA), ERC DBP ≥30 and ETCO2 ≥25; ERC pre-shock pause <5 s; adrenaline 1 mg every 3–5 min, as soon as feasible in non-shockable rhythms, after failed shocks (ERC: after the 3rd shock); vasopressin and high-dose adrenaline no benefit; amiodarone or lidocaine 2b; IV first (1, A), then IO; ET route removed; SGA or ETT; AF ≥200 J, flutter 200 J; all polymorphic VT treated as unstable; magnesium not for normal-QT VT; DSD and vector change of uncertain usefulness; TOR rules; ETCO2 <10 at 20 min in intubated patients only.
- **BLS and choking:** gasps in 40–60% of OHCA; lone rescuer with a phone calls and starts CPR; 5 back blows then 5 abdominal thrusts (adult and child; children's abdominal thrusts alone was the old rule); chest thrusts in late pregnancy or when the abdomen can't be encircled; infants get chest thrusts with the heel of one hand; unresponsive patient: CPR; no blind sweep; jaw thrust, then head tilt if it fails. The back-blow rationale (more relief, fewer injuries) is verbatim from AHA.
- **Special circumstances:** heat stroke: ice water 1–5 °C, ≥0.15 °C/min, stop at 38.6 °C, no dantrolene. Hypothermia below 30 °C (AHA): one shock, then defer further shocks and adrenaline; ECLS for arrest, and at <28 °C without arrest; ECLS rewarming 1.5–5 °C/h; HOPE <10%, ICE. Maternal arrest: resuscitative delivery (new AHA term) when the fundus is at or above the umbilicus, prepare at recognition, deliver by 5 min, done where she lies; manual LUD; upper-limb access; calcium for magnesium toxicity; remove fetal monitors; ECPR reasonable; AFE: massive transfusion protocol plus TXA. Asthma: ECLS, volatile agents, survival 83.5–100%, brief ventilator disconnection for breath stacking. Hyperkalaemic arrest: calcium, bicarbonate and insulin effect not established.
- **Trials:** Cheskes 2011 (n=815; preshock ≥20 vs <10 s OR 0.47; 18% per 5 s; perishock ≥40 vs <20 s OR 0.54, so the trap's figure is right). From memory, consistent: Paradis 1990 (n=100, no ROSC with CPP <15); ALPS (24.4/23.7/21.0%); PARAMEDIC-2 (8,014; 3.2 vs 2.4%; more severe disability); DOSE-VF (30.4/21.7/13.3%); ARREST (43 vs 7%, n=30); Prague (31.5 vs 22.0%, n=264); INCEPTION (20 vs 16%, n=134); HACA (55 vs 39%); Bernard (49 vs 26%); TTM; TTM2; HYPERION (10.2 vs 5.7%); BOX; COACT (64.5 vs 67.2%); TELSTAR; Kim 2014 (n=1,359).
- **Older values:** AHA adult choking was abdominal thrusts before 2025; cardioversion was 120–200 J for AF and 50–100 J for flutter (AHA 2015/2020).
- **Open, close and drill:** the open and close now agree with the steps (the close's six traps all match the lessons). All 25 drill answers are correct; the atropine answer now carries the ERC note.
