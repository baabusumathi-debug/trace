# AO (Aorta: dissection) — fact-check review, 23 Nov 2026

Sources: ESC 2024 PAAD guideline (Eur Heart J 2024;45:3538, ehae179: text and recommendation tables via academic.oup.com and orbi.uliege.be PDF); ACC/AHA 2022 aortic guideline (Circulation, CIR.0000000000001106); IRAD, Hagan JAMA 2000 (PMID 10685714); Sievers TEM, ICVTS 2020;30:451 (PMID 31755925); NYT 2006 (DeBakey's own type 2 dissection).

## Changes (where: old → new; source)
1. AD-11 lesson: "M three is spinal, visceral, renal or limb" → "M three is the visceral, renal or iliac arteries: the gut, the kidneys or the legs". Box M3 likewise, and the +/− meaning made explicit (+ clinical signs, − imaging only). Source: Sievers 2020 (M3 = visceral, renal or iliac; spinal cord not in the definition).
2. AD-16 lesson: D-dimer rule-out now named as part of the ESC algorithm with the ADD-RS (low risk = score zero or one). Source: ESC 2024 (multiparametric ADD-RS algorithm, I B; "below 500 ng/mL AAD is unlikely"). Spine line 4: "only in a low-risk patient" → "only with low risk (ADD-RS 0–1)".
3. AD-17 lesson: "You used the same split for pulmonary embolism in the last packet" → "It's the same split as in pulmonary embolism". The RV packet doesn't teach a stable-or-unstable imaging split, so the callback was false.
4. AD-21 lesson: antidote paragraph now also covers the older sodium nitrite plus thiosulfate kit (nitrite makes methaemoglobin on purpose) and says that thiocyanate has no antidote and is removed by haemodialysis. V12-032 answer line updated to match. Source: nitroprusside label and standard toxicology.
5. AD-12 lesson: after "one to two per cent per hour", added "Modern registry figures are lower, about a quarter at forty-eight hours, but the exam keeps fifty." Source: ESC 2024 citing IRAD, 23.7% medical vs 4.4% surgical. "In uncomplicated type B it does harm" → "it adds risk and no benefit".
6. Trap "Surgery improves survival in all dissections", truth: "Open surgery roughly doubles mortality…" → about 10–15% on drugs vs about 30% with open surgery, a figure inflated by selection; complicated type B gets TEVAR. The old wording claimed causation that the data don't show.
7. Close: "a systolic between one hundred and one hundred and twenty", "heart rate below sixty" → "a heart rate of about sixty or less and a systolic below one hundred and twenty". Source: ESC 2024 (SBP <120, HR ≤60, I B). The lower bound of 100 is not in the guideline, and the steps teach <120.

## Verified unchanged (selection)
- ESC 2024 targets: SBP <120 mmHg and HR ≤60 (I B); higher MAP allowed in spinal or brain ischaemia; IV beta-blocker first line (I B), labetalol first choice in the text, esmolol useful; non-DHP CCB if beta-blocker contraindicated (IIa B); vasodilator only after rate control.
- ACC/AHA 2022: HR 60–80, SBP <120; verapamil or diltiazem if beta-blocker intolerant; vasodilators (nicardipine, clevidipine, nitroprusside) never as initial treatment.
- Imaging: ECG-gated CT from neck to pelvis first line (I C), sensitivity 100% and specificity 98%; TOE if unstable or CT unavailable, and peri-operatively (I C).
- Type A: surgery; 1–2% per hour untreated in the first 48 h; peri-operative mortality 17–25%. Emergency pericardial puncture only as a temporary life-saving bridge before theatre. Valve resuspension preferred over replacement (I B); valved conduit for root destruction, root aneurysm or genetic aortopathy (I B).
- Type B: definition of complicated matches ESC; TEVAR first line in complicated disease (I B); high-risk features >40 mm, FL >20 mm, entry >10 mm; TEVAR at 14–90 days (IIa B, verbatim). Mesenteric malperfusion mortality 65–95%.
- TEM: E0 to E3, M0 to M2, the +/− signs, and the meaning of non-A non-B.
- IRAD: pulse deficit 15.1%, AR 31.6%, normal CXR 12.4%, normal ECG 31.3%; type A mortality 58% medical vs 26% surgical; type B 10.7% medical vs 31.4% surgical. Lesson figures within rounding.
- DeBakey 1965 and Stanford 1970. DeBakey's own type 2 dissection at 97, operated in February 2006.
- Doses: esmolol, labetalol, metoprolol, diltiazem, nicardipine, NTG and nitroprusside (0.3 to 10 mcg/kg/min, at most 10 min at maximum), 5 CN per molecule, cyanide risk above ~2 mcg/kg/min, thiocyanate toxic above ~60 mg/L.
- Drill: all 25 answers checked and correct. The open and close agree with the steps.
