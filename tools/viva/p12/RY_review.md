## Changes
1. **ACLS-27 lesson (sedation).** Old: "The current wording is sedation whenever feasible, even in the unstable patient, unless it would dangerously delay the shock." New: "The current guidance is that sedation is advisable in the awake patient, judged against how stable he is, and it must never dangerously delay the shock." Source: AHA 2025 ("appropriate sedation for cardioversion is advisable in awake patients"; mental status and stability decide). The same change is made in spine P12-501 line 5 ("Sedate whenever feasible" → "Sedate the awake patient where stability allows") and in trap "Mandatory intubation…" (truth).
2. **CAR-02 lesson (AVRT).** Old: "…and a delta wave shows between attacks." New: "…and, if the pathway conducts forwards in sinus rhythm, a delta wave shows between attacks." Why: a concealed pathway causes AVRT with a normal resting ECG. Mimic "AVNRT vs orthodromic AVRT" now reads "delta wave between attacks if the pathway is manifest."
3. **CAR-10 lesson (secondary-prevention ICD).** Old: "AVID, CIDS and CASH compared the ICD with amiodarone, and the ICD won on mortality." New: "…AVID showed a clear fall in mortality, the other two pointed the same way, and pooled together the ICD won." Why: only AVID was significant on its own; CIDS and CASH showed non-significant trends, and the pooled analysis showed the benefit (standard knowledge, not re-fetched). Trap "Amiodarone for secondary prevention…" truth changed to match.
4. **ACLS-37 lesson.** Old: "The two infusions drive the beta one receptor directly." New: "The two infusions stimulate the beta one receptor, so the rate rises whatever the vagus is doing." Why: dopamine acts partly indirectly, by releasing noradrenaline.
5. **CAR-06 lesson (torsades).** Added after "magnesium sulphate, two grams IV": "The American guideline grades it only as may be considered, yet it stays the exam answer." Source: AHA 2025 (COR 2b). To stay under 4,000 characters I removed the filler "Three more lines complete it."
6. **Brugada, arrhythmic syncope.** In ESC 2022, an ICD after a survived arrest is class I, and an ICD after arrhythmic syncope with a type 1 pattern is IIa. Spine P12-504 line 5: "→ ICD after arrest or syncope" → "→ ICD after arrest (class I) or arrhythmic syncope". Drill item 20 (Brugada): "ICD (class I), also after arrhythmic syncope" → "ICD (class I); after arrhythmic syncope ICD is IIa". Trap "Brugada arrest survivor" truth: "(and after arrhythmic syncope)" → "(IIa after arrhythmic syncope)". Source: ESC 2022, from recall (not re-fetched).
7. **Card flag CVS08B|ACLS-38, withdrawn.** The writer said the pack's "post-ROSC adrenaline 0.1–0.5 mcg/kg/min" belongs to noradrenaline. In fact the AHA 2015/2020 post-cardiac-arrest algorithm lists adrenaline 0.1–0.5 mcg/kg/min and noradrenaline 0.1–0.5 mcg/kg/min, so the pack figure is right. I rewrote the flag to say so (from recall, not re-fetched). Leaving it out of the lesson is still correct.
