## Sources fetched
- ESC/ERS 2022 PH guideline (Eur Heart J 43:3618, academic.oup.com): Table 6 group 5 is now 5.1 haematological (chronic haemolytic anaemia, myeloproliferative), 5.2 systemic (sarcoid, pulmonary LCH, NF1), 5.3 metabolic (glycogen storage, Gaucher), 5.4 chronic renal failure with or without dialysis, 5.5 pulmonary tumour thrombotic microangiopathy, 5.6 fibrosing mediastinitis. Congenital heart disease is in group 1 (1.4.4). Splenectomy and thyroid disease are no longer listed in the groups. CT: PA:aorta ratio above 0.9, PA diameter 30 mm or more. Severe PH in lung disease means a PVR above 5 WU. CTEPH: lifelong anticoagulation, VKA in antiphospholipid syndrome, PEA the treatment of choice, BPA for inoperable or residual disease, riociguat approved; medical therapy before BPA if PVR is above 4 WU; IV epoprostenol not part of the CTEPH ladder. Anticoagulation in PAH: not routine.
- 2026 AHA/ACC/ACCP/ACEP/CHEST/SCAI/SHM/SIR/SVM/SVN acute PE guideline (JACC 2026;87:1626, PMID 41712898; ACC journal scan; AHA newsroom). It exists, it is the first AHA/ACC PE guideline, and it introduces clinical categories A to E (A subclinical, B low severity, C–E in hospital, up to cardiopulmonary failure). Advanced therapies, IV or catheter lysis among them, are for categories D and E.
- Mekontso Dessap 2016, Intensive Care Med (QxMD abstract, PMID 26650055): 752 patients with moderate-to-severe ARDS on protective ventilation, acute cor pulmonale 22%; risk score of pneumonia, driving pressure 18 or more, P/F below 150, PaCO2 48 or more (all four correct in the lesson); hospital mortality not different for ACP overall, higher with severe ACP (57% vs 42%).
- Frontiers Cardiovasc Med 2023 review citing EHFS II: acute RV failure is 3–9% of AHF admissions, in-hospital mortality 5–17% (the lesson's figures are correct).

## Changes
1. **RVF-18 lesson (survival).** Old: "And survival is clearly better without it. In the largest echo series the excess deaths sat in severe acute cor pulmonale." New: "The card says survival is much higher without it, and that's the answer to mark. The detail behind it: in the largest echo series, of seven hundred and fifty-two patients, hospital mortality wasn't significantly different with acute cor pulmonale as a whole. The excess deaths sat in the severe form." Source: Mekontso Dessap 2016.
2. **RVF-21 lesson (CTEPH, epoprostenol).** Old: "By the guideline, epoprostenol is only for the critically ill." New: "The guideline's ladder for CTEPH doesn't include epoprostenol at all; at most it's a rescue for the critically ill." Source: ESC/ERS 2022 section 10.
3. **CAR-23 lesson (high-risk definition).** Old: "...obstructive shock; or persistent hypotension, meaning a systolic pressure below ninety, or a fall of forty or more, lasting longer than fifteen minutes, or a need for vasopressors." New: vasopressor need moved into obstructive shock, with signs of poor organ perfusion; persistent hypotension is a systolic pressure below ninety, or a fall of forty or more, for more than fifteen minutes. Source: ESC 2019 PE, Table 4.
4. **CAR-23 lesson (accelerated alteplase).** Old: "In cardiac arrest, the accelerated dose of zero point six milligrams per kilogram over fifteen minutes, to a maximum of fifty." New: "There's also an accelerated regimen, zero point six milligrams per kilogram over fifteen minutes, to a maximum of fifty, for when time is short, as in arrest." The old wording made arrest its only use. Source: ESC 2019 PE, Table 9.
5. **CAR-23 lesson (AHA/ACC 2026).** Old: "...relabels these as categories A to E, with E the hypotensive or arrested patient. The rule hasn't moved, and NEET will ask the European classes." New: "The first American guideline, from twenty twenty-six, relabels these as clinical categories A to E, running from the subclinical embolus at A up to cardiopulmonary failure at E. Advanced treatment, lysis included, is still kept for the sickest categories, and the bank keys the European classes." This removes the claim that the rule "hasn't moved" (category D, which is normotensive with incipient failure, can also get advanced therapy) and the prediction about NEET. Source: AHA/ACC 2026 pages above.
6. **Close (P12-804).** Old: "defined by a mean pressure above twenty, and a resistance above two Wood units". New: "defined by a mean pressure above twenty, pre-capillary disease adds a resistance above two Wood units". PVR above 2 WU belongs to the pre-capillary definition, not to PH itself. Source: ESC/ERS 2022.
7. **Card flag RVF-39.** Source corrected: the ">200 mL over 15–30 min" figure comes from the 2016 HFA/ESC statement on acute RV failure (Harjola), not an "ESC 2016 heart failure" guideline.
8. **Card flag CAR-23.** Reworded to the verified content of the AHA/ACC 2026 guideline (see final flags).
