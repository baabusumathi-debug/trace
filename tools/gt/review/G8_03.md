## GT8-53 — CCB poisoning / high-dose insulin
- Changed: the AHA line. High-dose insulin is class 1 for **hypotension** in calcium-channel blocker poisoning and in beta-blocker poisoning (not only "refractory shock"), and VA-ECMO is class 2a. Source: 2023 AHA focused update on poisoning — https://www.emdocs.net/2023-aha-update-on-management-cardiac-arrest-or-life-threatening-toxicity-due-to-poisoning/ (summarising Lavonas et al., Circulation 2023, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37721023/).
- Mechanism (shift to carbohydrate fuel, blocked calcium-dependent insulin release, insulin resistance, hyperglycaemia as a severity marker) and dosing left as written.

## GT8-55 — QTc in organophosphate poisoning
- Changed: the evidence claim. The writer said a long QTc is associated with "larger atropine requirements, more respiratory failure and higher mortality". The mortality association is solid (mortality 34.9% vs 4.0%; multivariate OR 7.46, 95% CI 2.05–27.10) but in that same series atropine use and respiratory failure did **not** differ by QTc group. Rewritten to teach the mortality association with its numbers, flag the atropine association as a weaker, series-dependent claim, and drop respiratory failure. Option B and `key.note` adjusted to match. Source: Chen et al., PLoS One — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3344908
- Key D ("may indicate increased severity") remains correct, and the hedge is now properly earned rather than asserted.
- Peradeniya scale components and the torsades management left unchanged.
