# GI01C review (independent fact-check, 20 Nov 2026)

12 cards, 12 entries; check_learn.py GI01C: OK (v3 spine 6 steps OK). No Top-up cards for GI01C.

## Changes
- ALF-34a: "factor V has the shortest half-life of the vitamin K–independent factors" (wrong: factor VIII's half-life is shorter) → factor V is made only by hepatocytes, half-life roughly 12–36 h, vitamin K–independent (standard haematology; Pereira, Gut 1992).
- ALF-35a: double weight of HE and ammonia given as "because cerebral oedema is the main killer" → they were the strongest predictors in the derivation model, which fits a population where cerebral oedema kills (Kumar, Gut 2012). Minor.
- ALF-36a: risk-group mortalities unsourced → states they are the Kumar 2012 cohorts, later quoted as about 2.6% / 33% / 88.5% (PMC6970204). The card's 3%, 29–37%, 80–96% fit these. Settled.
- ALF-41c: "one plasma volume is about 40 mL/kg, so 1.5–2 volumes is about 3–5 L" (the arithmetic gives 4–6 L) → about 4–6 L per session. Minor.
- ALF-43a: "bleeding complications in about one in ten" → haemorrhage in 10.3% of 58 monitored patients, half incidental on imaging, possibly contributing to 2 deaths; 30-day post-transplant survival 85% in both groups (Vaquero, Liver Transpl 2005). Card's 10.3% confirmed.
- ALF-43c: "in its trial it delayed herniation without reducing oedema or deaths" → the controlled trial (Ede 1986) did not reduce cerebral oedema or improve survival. The "delayed herniation" claim could not be verified and was removed; "no effect on cerebral oedema" is confirmed by reviews citing the trial (PMC3338232). Also added why the effect fades (CSF bicarbonate readjusts).
- ALF-43e: LOLA mechanism ("residual … glutamine synthesis capacity, which a necrotic liver lacks"; muscle also makes glutamine) → the urea cycle cannot respond after massive hepatocyte loss, and glutamine made in muscle is broken back down to ammonia by glutaminase in gut and kidney. Factual (mechanism).
- ALF-42b: "about two-thirds died" (unsure) → 67% died: 70 of 1223 ALF patients; predictors bilirubin, PT prolongation, grade 3–4 HE at presentation (Kumar, Hepatology 2010; 67.1%). Settled.
- ALF-32d: "more than half recover without transplant" for paracetamol, HAV, ischaemic, pregnancy → "about half or more" (pregnancy-related and HAV are close to half in ALFSG). Minor.
- FLAG ALF-32: now gives the source. Acharya 1996 says about 75% of deaths came within 72 h. Overall mortality was 66%, which is likely where "two-thirds" came from.
- FLAG ALF-41: records the second failed attempt to retrieve the ACG 2023 plasma-exchange wording.
- Header: added a review line listing the sources confirmed online.

## Settled (no text change needed)
- Factor VIII/V ratio >30, paracetamol (Pereira, Gut 1992: >30 in 10 of 11 deaths; all survivors <30). Correct.
- APACHE II >15, paracetamol (Mitchell, Crit Care Med 1998, 102 patients: sensitivity 82%, specificity 98%; similar to King's). Correct.
- SCCM 2023 wording on plasma exchange (hyperammonaemia >150 µmol/L), extracorporeal support, ICP monitoring, hypertonic saline and ammonia-lowering agents in ALF. Matches the text.
- ACG 2023: King's or MELD for prognosis; meeting King's or MELD >25 = high risk (conditional, low); no conclusive evidence for routine ICP monitoring. Matches.
- Acharya 1996 (423 patients; 4 predictors; ≥3 present → 93% died). Matches.
- Traps checked on their truth side: Budd–Chiari/cancer, King's sorting, bilirubin 10 vs 17.5, MELD as least-wrong, factor V = Clichy, ALFED dynamic, ELAD human C3A, MARS P 0.46, plasma exchange not helping as a bridge. All hold.

## Could not verify
- ACG 2023 wording on plasma exchange. The full text is paywalled, and no summary or slide set mentions it. The card's "evidence still insufficient" stays as printed and flagged.
- Ede 1986 survival and herniation details (abstract not retrievable). Kept only "no benefit".

## Final FLAGS
- ALF-32: Marrow key (transplant evaluation for fulminant Budd–Chiari despite colon cancer 2 months earlier) conflicts with c (extrahepatic malignancy contraindicates); the trap gives the card-layer rule. Watch line "two-thirds of deaths within 72 h": Acharya 1996 says about 75% (66% was overall mortality).
- ALF-40: HepatAssist randomised 171; 147 = after excluding primary non-function. "No difference" holds overall (71% vs 62%, P 0.26), but the adjusted fulminant/subfulminant subgroup showed benefit (RR 0.56, P 0.048).
- ALF-41: "ACG 2023: evidence still insufficient" unverifiable (paywalled; not in any summary). EASL 2017 (I,1) and SCCM 2023 (conditional) support plasma exchange. b omits that the 2022 trial was in ALF with cerebral oedema, with a 21-day endpoint.
