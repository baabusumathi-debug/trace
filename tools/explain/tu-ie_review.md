## Changes
1. **IE-08 e (factual).** Before: the vegetation ≥10 mm + severe valve disease row and the ≥30 mm row were called "weaker recommendations" next to IIb. Now: S. aureus or non-HACEK Gram-negative PVE is still IIa C. Vegetation ≥10 mm with any other surgical indication was upgraded to Class I C in 2023. The 30 mm cut-off was dropped. An isolated vegetation ≥10 mm in a low-risk patient is IIb B (in 2015 it was >15 mm, IIb C). Sources: the ESC 2023 tables as reproduced in the lecture; Rev Esp Cardiol ("upgraded to class I"); J Cardiothorac Surg 2024.
2. **IE-08 b (minor).** Before: "ESC 2023 shortened urgent to 3–5 days". Now: "defines urgent as 3–5 days (2015 said only 'within a few days')". Source: ACC summary.
3. **IE-09 f (factual, omission).** Before: only "stop all anticoagulation in ICH". Now it adds what ESC 2023 says:
   - antithrombotics are stopped in major bleeding (I C);
   - with a mechanical valve, UFH is restarted as soon as possible after an ICH, after team discussion (IIa C);
   - the fixed "UFH for 1–2 weeks after ischaemic stroke" is ESC 2015 wording.
   Source: the ESC 2023 antithrombotic table as reproduced in the lecture.
4. **IE-10 trap (factual).** Before: called vancomycin + ceftriaxone "the community or late-PVE choice". It is not an ESC 2023 empirical regimen. 5. **IE-12 d (factual, softened).** Before: "response within 96 h" as an ESC condition for the 2-week right-sided MSSA course. I could not verify this wording. 6. **IE-12 b (minor).** Before: "ESC 2023 insists on … a second antibiotic". Now: ESC uses daptomycin 10 mg/kg and advises a second agent with it (for example cloxacillin or fosfomycin) to prevent resistance.

## Settled as correct (no change)
- **Surgery timing.**
  - Emergency within 24 h: refractory oedema or shock (I B).
  - Urgent 3–5 days: HF (I B) and locally uncontrolled infection including new AV block (I B).
  - Urgent or non-urgent: fungi or multiresistant organisms (I C).
  - Urgent, should be considered: persistent positive cultures for more than 1 week (IIa B).
- **Neurology.**
  - Surgery without delay after TIA or non-haemorrhagic stroke (I B).
  - After ICH: delay at least 4 weeks (more than 1 month); if the patient is unstable and a meaningful neurological recovery is likely, consider earlier surgery (IIa C).
  - Thrombolysis is contraindicated (III C).
- **POET:** 9.0% vs 12.1% at 6 months; 26.4% vs 38.2% at a median of 3.5 years. A TEE showing no progression is needed before switching to oral (ACC summary).
- **Duke-ISCVID:** thresholds, PET-CT timing, and how the worked case in IE-04 is counted.
- **Antibiotics:** the AHA 2015 viridans MIC bands, ceftriaxone monotherapy, and the enterococcal, HACEK, Pseudomonas (tobramycin 8 mg/kg/day), Burkholderia, Coxiella, Bartonella and Candida regimens.
