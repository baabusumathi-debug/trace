## Changes

- **PCA-28 (part d, table, FLAG) — factual:** the explanation and FLAG said the card's "ERC/ESICM 2021: constant 32–36 °C for ≥24 h" was really 2015 wording and that 2021 recommended only fever prevention. That was wrong. ERC–ESICM 2021 (Nolan 2021) does say: constant 32–36 °C for at least 24 h, and avoid fever >37.7 °C for at least 72 h. The fever-prevention-only position came in the ERC–ESICM 2022 temperature update after TTM2 (Sandroni, ICM 2022). The explanation now says this. The table now has separate 2021 and 2022 rows. The FLAG now covers only the card's "TTM at 36 °C" wording, which the guideline did not single out (source: esanum ERC 2021 summary; PubMed 35089409).
- **PCA-28 (trap) — factual:** RINSE was grouped with Kim under "more re-arrest and pulmonary oedema". RINSE gave cold saline during CPR and lowered ROSC in shockable rhythms (41.2% vs 50.6%, P = .03), with no survival difference. The trap now says this; the Kim findings are kept (PubMed 27562972).
- **PCA-21 (b) — factual/minor:** CMRO₂ fall "6–7% per °C" → "6–10% per °C", the usual figure from Polderman's reviews.
- **PCA-34 (b) — factual/minor:** buspirone "lowers the shivering threshold by about a degree" was not supported → "lowers it modestly on its own and acts synergistically with meperidine" (Mokhtarani 2001).
- **PCA-36 (b) — minor:** meperidine "acts on kappa receptors" → it lowers the shivering threshold about twice as much as the vasoconstriction threshold, an effect put down partly to kappa-opioid and alpha-2 actions (PMC5882245 review).
- **PCA-36 (mimic) — minor:** added that Hofmann elimination itself slows a little in the cold, so the text now matches the card-layer line.
- **PCA-24 (d) — minor:** the unverified "target reached in about 10 hours" → "several hours after ROSC".
- **PCA-23 (decode) — minor:** NNT "every 6 treated" → "every 4–6". HACA's NNT is about 6 and Bernard's about 4.
- **PCA-30 (e) and new FLAG — minor:** added that published cooling rates vary between sources. In the head-to-head ICU study (Hoedemaekers, Crit Care 2007) devices cooled at about 1–1.5 °C/h and cold fluid plus ice at about 0.3 °C/h, so the reader should learn the card's ranking, not the decimals.
