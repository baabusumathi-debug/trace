# TOX06 review — independent fact-check (5 Nov)

## Changes
- ONC-04 b: CRS incidence given only as card's 80–85% with "rates differ" → adds the real spread (~60% to >90% any grade across pivotal trials, highest with CD28-based axi-cel). Source: ZUMA-1, JULIET, ELIANA; IBCC CAR-T chapter.
- ONC-05 c: invented rationale "vasopressin not counted because fixed-dose adjunct" → states it is excluded by the ASTCT consensus; adds the >6 L/min / Venturi detail for grade 3 hypoxia. Source: Lee et al., BBMT 2019 (ASTCT).
- ONC-06 b: "dexamethasone added if hypotension persists" (no dose) → dexamethasone 10 mg IV 12–24-hourly after fluids and 1–2 tocilizumab doses. Source: ASCO 2021 / NCCN as summarised by IBCC and UIC DIG.
- ONC-06 c: adds siltuximab is off-label; dexamethasone 10 mg 6-hourly with escalation if refractory. Source: same.
- ONC-06 d: adds that many protocols start with dexamethasone 10 mg 6-hourly and move to methylprednisolone 1 g/day ×3 days then taper. Source: IBCC; NCCN.
- ONC-08 intro: unverifiable "done at least twice a day" → "repeated several times a day while at risk".
- ONC-08 a: unsupported claim that orientation/naming carry most points "because most frequently affected" → each task explained by what it tests (encephalopathy, expressive aphasia, dysgraphia, attention). Source: Lee 2019.
- ONC-08 mimic: added CAPD replaces ICE under 12 years. Source: Lee 2019 (ASTCT).
- ONC-09 b: vague "dexamethasone considered" → dexamethasone 10 mg IV once, repeat 6–12-hourly if no improvement. Source: NCCN via IBCC.
- ONC-09 d: "immunosuppression is escalated" → methylprednisolone 1 g IV daily ×3 days for grade 4 (ASCO/NCCN). Source: IBCC; UIC DIG summary of ASCO/NCCN.
- ONC-09 table: grade 3 seizure now includes non-convulsive EEG seizures settling with treatment; grade 4 "repeated without recovery". Source: Lee 2019.
- ONC-10 b: 1–2 mg/kg presented as universal → noted as the grade 3–4 dose; grade 2 is 0.5–1 mg/kg. Source: ASCO 2021 irAE guideline (Schneider).
- ONC-10 c: explanation silent on the outdated 5–7-day window → states current ASCO/ESMO steroid-refractory threshold ~48–72 h of IV steroids and why; adds vedolizumab. Source: ASCO 2021; ESMO 2022 (Haanen); SITC 2023 (via Pharmacy Times).
- ONC-10 e: "stop permanently after grade 3 or 4" explained without nuance → grade 4 and any-grade myocarditis are permanent; some grade 3 events (e.g. anti-PD-1 colitis) allow rechallenge after recovery to grade ≤1. Source: ASCO 2021.
- ONC-11 a: sunitinib presented as an established cause of GBS/myasthenia → noted as case-report evidence only. Source: Case Rep Oncol Med 2014 (GBS after sunitinib).
- ONC-11 d: myocarditis lacked dose and next steps → methylprednisolone 500–1000 mg/day for 3–5 days, early, drug stopped permanently; mycophenolate/abatacept if refractory. Source: ESC 2022 cardio-oncology guideline; ASCO 2021.
- ONC-11 f: trastuzumab accepted as an ACS drug → explains trastuzumab causes reversible LV systolic dysfunction (HER2 survival signalling in cardiomyocytes, worse after anthracyclines), not ACS; adds 5-FU/capecitabine vasospasm (stop infusion, nitrate/CCB) and bevacizumab arterial thrombosis. Added table rows, a trap and two decode items. Source: ESC 2022 cardio-oncology guideline; Scappaticci JNCI 2007 (bevacizumab ATE).
- ONC-13 g: vague "pituitary is a classic target" → mechanism: pituitary cells express CTLA-4, antibody binding fixes complement in the gland. Source: Iwama et al., Sci Transl Med 2014.
- FLAGS ONC-06: writer said NCI triggers "could not be verified" → verified (LVEF <40%, creatinine >2.5× baseline, noradrenaline >2 µg/min for 48 h; Kroschinsky 2017 Crit Care Table 3); flag now says they are correct but pre-ASTCT/historical.
- FLAGS ONC-10: "many grade 3 events (colitis or hepatitis)" → "some grade 3 events (e.g. anti-PD-1 colitis)"; hepatitis rechallenge rules are stricter and not verified.
- FLAGS ONC-11: added sunitinib (case reports only) and myocarditis row omitting permanent discontinuation.

Checked and left unchanged (correct): ASTCT CRS grades 1–4; fever ≥38 °C and grading after antipyretics/tocilizumab; ICE domains 4/3/1/1/1 and grade bands 7–9 / 3–6 / 0–2 / 0 unarousable; ICANS domains (consciousness, seizure, motor, ICP/oedema); tocilizumab 8 mg/kg max 800 mg, q8h, max 3 in 24 h and 4 total; tocilizumab not for isolated ICANS; IL-6 trans-signalling, Ang-2/vWF; hydrocortisone before thyroxine in hypophysitis; mycophenolate not infliximab for ICI hepatitis; ICI AIN median onset ~3 months with PPI co-trigger; anti-VEGF renal TMA; cetuximab hypomagnesaemia; dasatinib effusion/PAH.

## Final FLAGS
- ONC-06: Tocilizumab "maximum 3 doses" — current guidance is max 3 in 24 h and 4 in total. NCI triggers in the WATCH are correctly quoted but historical (pre-ASTCT).
- ONC-09: Grade 4 ICANS "as 3" implies dexamethasone; ASCO/NCCN use methylprednisolone 1 g/day (usually 3 days).
- ONC-10: (c) "5–7 days" is outdated — steroid-refractory is ~48–72 h. (e) Permanent discontinuation is the rule for grade 4 and any-grade myocarditis; some grade 3 events allow rechallenge after recovery to grade ≤1.
- ONC-11: Trastuzumab is not an ACS drug (reversible LV dysfunction); ACS drugs are bevacizumab and 5-FU/capecitabine. Sunitinib–GBS/myasthenia is case-report level. Myocarditis row omits permanent discontinuation and the pulse dose.
