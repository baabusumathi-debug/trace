# OBS01 review (independent fact-check, 12 Nov 2026)

Checked all 14 cards and the asthma top-up (T_82a35a02) against the card text, qpack/OBS01.md (20 linked questions), the DocT GT source questions and the references below. Checker: `OBS01 14 cards 14 entries OK`, `v3 spine 5 steps OK`.

## Changes (card: what was wrong → what it says now; source)

- OBS-08c: said an apnoeic term woman reaches SpO2 90% "in about 3 minutes or less, roughly half the time of a non-pregnant adult" (too short, ratio overstated) → about 5 minutes on modelling against about 7½ minutes non-pregnant, faster once below 90%, shorter in labour, obesity, sepsis or poor pre-oxygenation. Source: McClelland et al., Anaesthesia 2009 (median 4 min 52 s vs 7 min 25 s), PubMed 18289232.
- OBS-05e (+ header comment, FLAG): base excess given as "about −2 to −4 mmol/L", a figure the writer derived and no source prints → softened to "mildly negative, a base deficit of a few mmol/L". Sources checked: Deranged Physiology, OpenAnesthesia, Brandis Acid-Base Physiology 11.1 (none gives a base-excess figure).
- T_82a35a02 b (+ FLAG): mild asthma presented as SABA alone with no caveat → added that GINA since 2019 does not recommend reliever-only treatment for any adult, pregnant included; "inhaled β2-agonist" is the DocT key from the older NAEPP tiers. Source: GINA strategy reports 2019 onward. FLAG extended to say the top-up line could be reworded.
- OBS-09b: restated the card's upper yellow temperature band (37.5–38 °C) with no comment although part c already corrected the systolic band → added that the published chart has no upper yellow band (35–36 yellow; over 38 red). Source: Singh 2012 / CEMACH 2007 chart as reproduced in Friedman 2018, PMC5910060 (re-read today).
- OBS-09 decode (pulse 125, RR 22, temp 37.8): answer leaned on the temperature being yellow, which is true only on the card → "one red is enough on its own, and the respiratory rate is yellow as well". Same source.
- OBS-13c: "maternal sepsis or critical illness is not a reason to omit them" was too absolute → "not in itself a contraindication when preterm delivery is expected; delivery the mother needs is not delayed to complete the course". Source: SMFM Consult Series maternal sepsis; ACOG CO 713.
- OBS-02 mimic (AFE vs PE): "DIC … often within minutes" unverifiable timing → "early DIC and bleeding, which may be the presenting feature; pulmonary embolism does not cause a consumptive coagulopathy". Source: SMFM AFE guidance 2016 / Clark criteria.
- OBS-02b: ketoacidosis in pregnancy attributed only to the low bicarbonate buffer (incomplete mechanism) → insulin resistance from placental hormones, continuous fetoplacental glucose uptake (early fasting ketosis) and the low buffer. Source: standard obstetric medicine texts (de Swiet; Sibai DKA in pregnancy review 2014).
- OBS-04d: bold line says rate rises while part a says rate changes little → added "the rise in rate is small (a breath or two a minute); depth does most of the work", and CO2 production alongside oxygen consumption. Source: Hegewald & Crapo 2011.
- OBS-14 trap: CTPA dose "about a hundred times below" 50 mGy (0.66 mGy is 75 times below) → "at least 75 times below". OBS-14b: "falls at the bottom of this band" → "at or below the bottom" (0.01 mGy is below 0.1). Source: ACOG Committee Opinion 723 (2017) table.
- OBS-02 FLAG added (Marfan aortic root threshold; see below). Source: ESC 2018 cardiovascular disease in pregnancy, modified WHO classes.

## Final FLAGS

- OBS-01: pie chart (pre-eclampsia/eclampsia 75%, medical 14%, haemorrhage 11%) matched by position in the scan, not verifiable; proportions vary between series.
- OBS-02 (added): watch line "Marfan with aortic root over 4 cm" is the older threshold; ESC 2018 puts 40–45 mm in mWHO class III and over 45 mm in class IV.
- OBS-03: card gives antenatal figures only; DocT keys 60–80% for "active labour" (texts: about +50% second stage, 60–80% immediately post partum); card could add this and the normal auscultation. Oxygen consumption "up 30–40%" is the upper end of published figures.
- OBS-05: card prints base excess "no change"; it is mildly negative (small base deficit) but no checked source gives an exact figure.
- OBS-08: "2.5 to 3 times more difficult" is the notes' multiplier; audit figures (1 in 390 obstetric, Kinsella 2015, vs about 1 in 2000) give a larger ratio. The GT numbers in the watch lines of OBS-04 and OBS-08 (GT-4 Q29, GT-3 Q47) are asthma-in-pregnancy questions in the DocT file (confirmed), so the citations are wrong.
- OBS-09: card's systolic bands (yellow 150–180, red over 180) differ from the published chart (yellow 150–160 or 90–100; red over 160 or under 90); published chart has no upper yellow temperature band and makes SpO2 under 95% red. Diastolic red "over 100" is correct.
- OBS-11: card faithful to OAA/DAS 2015; NEET 2025 key (cricothyrotomy with difficult, not impossible, mask ventilation) is disputed.
- OBS-12: DocT key 8–9 mL/kg is wrong; card states no tidal volume and could add 6 mL/kg PBW. Watch line PaCO2 30–32 vs qbank 30–45. Card c's reason for prone is the notes' wording.
- OBS-13: NEET 2025 key phenylephrine holds for spinal hypotension only; noradrenaline for shock. Steroid window and dexamethasone alternative come from guidance, not the card.
- T_82a35a02 (top-up): consistent with guidance except that "mild → inhaled SABA" follows the older NAEPP tiers; GINA since 2019 advises against SABA-only treatment. DocT GT3 Q149 is mis-keyed; DocT's severe tier lists sustained-release theophylline, now rarely used.
