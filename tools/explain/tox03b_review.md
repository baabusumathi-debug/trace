# TOX03B review (second check)

## Changes
- PEST-23 c (factual, depth): text gave only the plasma-vs-tissue reasoning and the writer's flag called the card's source unverifiable → now names the source and adds the finding that explains it: 2017 meta-analysis, 3 RCTs, 290 patients, CVVH added to standard care, mortality about 58% vs 61% (RR 0.94, 95% CI 0.78–1.15); CVVH prolonged survival and shifted deaths from circulatory to respiratory failure. Card confirmed correct. Source: Lin G et al., Medicine 2017;96:e6875 (PMID 28514303).
- PEST-23 b (factual, depth): "none has been shown to reliably improve survival" with no evidence → now states early small steroid + cyclophosphamide trials looked promising; largest RCT (Sri Lanka, n=299, 2018) found no significant benefit (in-hospital mortality 53% vs 62%, p=0.12); 2021 Cochrane review (4 trials, 463 patients) low certainty, in-hospital RR 0.82, 3-month RR 0.98. Sources: Gawarammana et al., Clin Toxicol 2018;56:633–639; Cochrane CD008084.pub5 (2021).
- PEST-18 d (factual): "hypomagnesaemia ... common, but trials have not shown" → low magnesium reported in some series, small trials conflict, no survival benefit established (Chugh 1991 reported benefit; Siwach 1994 none). Trap correction softened to match. Source: standard toxicology teaching (Goldfrank's); trial conflict as above.
- PEST-18 mimic (factual): "phosphide patient is dry" (not true: vomiting and sweating are common) → OP has a cholinergic toxidrome (miosis, bronchorrhoea, bradycardia, fasciculations); phosphide has shock and acidosis without it.
- PEST-16 c (minor, reasoning): "plasma exchange does not restore conjugation, which is why bilirubin did not change" stated as fact → softened to a likely reason (continuing failure to conjugate and excrete, tissue stores re-equilibrate, so exchange lowers plasma bilirubin only briefly).
- FLAGS PEST-23 removed: its doubt about the RR 0.94 meta-analysis was wrong (source found, above). Its Cochrane RR 0.72 figure was from the 2014 version, now replaced by the 2021 update.
- FLAGS PEST-18 extended: magnesium "unproven" is fair because trials conflict.

Everything else checked and kept: Celphos 3 g tablet → about 1 g phosphine; yellow phosphorus lethal dose about 1 mg/kg; brodifacoum bait 0.005%; fluorocitrate/aconitase; factor VII half-life about 6 h; paraquat 10–20 mL of 20% = 2–4 g; Vale grades (<20, 20–40, >40 mg/kg); dithionite blue (diquat green); SIPP (<10 survival, Sawada 1988); Proudfoot 50% survival points 2.0/0.6/0.3/0.16/0.1 mg/L at 4/6/10/16/24 h.

## Final FLAGS
- PEST-14: Bromethalin grouping varies by source; thallium and barium carbonate missing from "highly toxic". Card kept.
- PEST-15: "Shock within 30 minutes" is compressed (shock usually 1–6 h, most deaths within 24 h); classic yellow-phosphorus staging is GI (first 24 h), latent (24–72 h), hepatorenal failure after 72 h. Card kept.
- PEST-16: Notes' "vegetable oil lavage" probably garbled liquid paraffin; lavage not routine in current guidance. Card kept.
- PEST-17: Chlorophacinone is an indandione, not covered by a "4-hydroxycoumarin level"; INR rises at 24–48 h but clinical bleeding often later; "below 30%" not verified. Card kept.
- PEST-18: 26% PGI figure not verified independently; magnesium "unproven" is fair because trials conflict.
- PEST-19: Fatal poisoning has been reported after prolonged skin contact with concentrate on damaged skin. Card kept.
