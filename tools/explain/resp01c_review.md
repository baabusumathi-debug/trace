# RESP01C review — independent fact-check (8 Nov)

## Changes
- ARDS-30 c: “ESICM 2023, ATS recommend prone ≥16 h a day in moderate-to-severe ARDS” — ATS does not say 16 h → ESICM 2023 more than 16 h a day in moderate-to-severe ARDS; ATS more than 12 h a day in severe ARDS (strong). Source: ESICM 2023 (Grasselli, ICM 2023); ATS/ESICM/SCCM 2017 (Fan, AJRCCM 2017), carried into the 2024 ATS summary.
- ARDS-30 trap: attributed a “steroids have no survival benefit” line to a Marrow explanation that is not in the qpack → trap rebuilt on the real DocT GT8 distractor (methylprednisolone 2 mg/kg/day): prone has the stronger, more consistent mortality evidence; early steroids are conditionally recommended (ATS 2024, SCCM 2024) on weaker evidence. FLAGS line about the Marrow explanation removed for the same reason.
- ARDS-33 c: guideline line incomplete → both ESICM 2023 and ATS 2024 recommend strongly against prolonged high-pressure recruitment manoeuvres; ESICM also suggests (weakly) against brief ones. Source: ATS 2024 update (Qadir, AJRCCM 2024); ESICM 2023; bedside comparison, PMC12040422. ART figures checked and kept: 1010 analysed, 28-day mortality 55.3% vs 49.3% (HR 1.20, P = 0.041), pneumothorax needing drainage 3.2% vs 1.2% (Cavalcanti, JAMA 2017).
- ARDS-34 c: “ATS 2017 and ESICM 2023 recommend against routine HFOV” — ESICM 2023 did not address HFOV → “ATS/ESICM/SCCM 2017 guideline makes a strong recommendation against routine HFOV; the 2023 ESICM guideline did not revisit it.” Source: Fan AJRCCM 2017; PMC12040422 table (ESICM 2023: not addressed). OSCILLATE 47% vs 35% in-hospital (RR 1.33, P = 0.005; 548 randomised) and OSCAR 41.7% vs 41.1% at 30 days (795) verified.
- ARDS-35 a: older ELSO threshold stated loosely → P/F <100 on FiO₂ >0.9 for ≥6 h despite optimal care = mortality risk ≥80% (ECMO indicated); P/F <150 = ~50% (consider). Source: ELSO adult respiratory failure guideline v1.3 (2013).
- ARDS-35 trap: “EOLIA thresholds apply despite optimal ventilation and prone” — EOLIA required optimised ventilation (FiO₂ ≥0.8, VT 6 mL/kg, PEEP ≥10) but did not mandate prone → “ECMO thresholds are read as ‘despite optimal ventilation’, which includes prone.” EOLIA inclusion (P/F <50 for >3 h; <80 for >6 h; pH <7.25 with PaCO₂ ≥60 for >6 h, RR 35, Pplat ≤32) verified. Source: Combes NEJM 2018 via Critical Care Reviews / The Bottom Line.
- ARDS-36 mimic: “0.07 on the survival curve” made precise → 0.07 is the time-to-death analysis to day 60, HR 0.70 (95% CI 0.47–1.04). Primary RR 0.76 (0.55–1.04), P = 0.09; 28% crossover; stopped at 249/331 for futility; treatment failure RR 0.62, P <0.001 — all verified. Source: Combes NEJM 2018; SCCM Concise Critical Appraisal.
- ARDS-38 b: “benefit after adjustment” → added that crude 90-day mortality 31.6% vs 40.7% was not significant (P = 0.08), so the benefit exists only after adjustment. Source: Papazian NEJM 2010. ESICM 2023 (strong against routine continuous NMBA infusion) and ATS 2024 (conditional for NMBA in early severe ARDS) verified and kept.
- ARDS-40 e: “now conditionally recommend corticosteroids in ARDS, started early” — neither guideline sets a start-time rule → “on the evidence of trials that started them early.” Source: ATS 2024; SCCM 2024 focused update. DEXA-ARDS verified and kept: 277 patients, P/F ≤200 on PEEP ≥10 and FiO₂ ≥0.5, 60-day mortality 21% vs 36% (−15.3%), VFD +4.8, stopped for low enrolment (planned 314), clinicians not blinded (Villar, Lancet Respir Med 2020).
- ARDS-43 a: “published figures range from about a third to over a half” → about 15% to 60%, most a third to a half (Sutherland 1995 15%, Markowicz 2000 36%, Meduri 1998 43%, Chastre 1998 55%, Delclaux 1997 60%). FLAGS ARDS-43 updated with the same range.
- ARDS-44 a: “mostly young adults without major illness” (unverified) → “relatively young adults (median age in the mid-40s)”. Source: Herridge NEJM 2003/2011.

Checked and left unchanged (correct): PROSEVA (466 patients; P/F <150 on FiO₂ ≥0.6, PEEP ≥5, VT ~6 mL/kg after 12–24 h; ventilated <36 h; sessions ≥16 consecutive h, mean 17 ± 3 h; 28-day 16.0% vs 32.8%, 90-day 23.6% vs 41.0%); oxygenation response not predicting survival (PROSEVA post hoc, Albert AJRCCM 2014); earlier prone trials ~7 h/day; EPVent-2 negative; CESAR (180; 63% vs 47%, RR 0.69, P = 0.03; 75% of the ECMO arm received ECMO); later Bayesian/pooled ECMO analyses; ATS 2024 conditional for VV-ECMO; ROSE (1006; 90-day ~43% in both arms; stopped for futility; higher-PEEP strategy); FACTT (1000; CVP <4/PAOP <8 vs CVP 10–14/PAOP 14–18; −136 vs +6992 mL; 60-day 25.5% vs 28.4%; VFD 14.6 vs 12.1; PAC vs CVC 27.4% vs 26.3%); Meduri 2007 (91 patients, 1 mg/kg/day ≤72 h, ICU mortality 20.6% vs 42.9%); LaSRS (180, day 7–28, no 60/180-day difference, more VFD/shock-free days, more neuromyopathy, ≥14 days 35% vs 8% and 44% vs 12%); RECOVERY subgroups; EDEN and OMEGA results; BALTI-2, ALTA, SAILS, HARP-2, KGF, LIPS-A; iNO and AKI in meta-analyses; ARMA 31% vs 40%; acute cor pulmonale ~22–25%; Herridge 5-year 109 survivors, 6MWD 436 m (76%); every cohort % and key claim against the qpack.

## Final FLAGS
- ARDS-30: Card right. One DocT GT paper (AIM2 Q97) prints the keyed prone option as ‘≤16 hours/day’ — misprint for ≥16 h.
- ARDS-32: Card right. DocT GT (CGT4) has two true options: the keyed mechanism statement and ‘proning is contraindicated in spinal fractures’.
- ARDS-35: Card right. DocT/DocT GT key ECMO for refractory ARDS when prone is also offered and untried — guidelines put prone first. DocT’s ‘P/F <100’ is the older ELSO threshold, not EOLIA’s <80 for 6 h. The polytrauma key note says pH 7.25 with PaCO₂ 68 meets EOLIA; strictly EOLIA needs pH <7.25 — the vignette qualifies on P/F 67 for 6 h instead.
- ARDS-42: WATCH ‘No drug has consistently reduced mortality’: dexamethasone reduced mortality in RECOVERY and DEXA-ARDS, and early corticosteroids are conditionally recommended (ATS 2024, SCCM 2024); the Harrison grade table rating steroids D is dated.
- ARDS-43: ‘VAP about 60%’ is the upper end of published series (about 15–60%, most a third to a half); kept with that caveat.
