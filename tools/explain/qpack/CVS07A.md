## PCA-17 · Give the haemodynamic management of the post-arrest patient: — 1 questions

- **[DocT GT · GT8]** A 58-year-old man is resuscitated after a cardiac arrest. He remains comatose in the ICU. Targeted temperature management is initiated. On day 1, his MAP is 65 mm Hg despite fluids. What is the next best step?
  - Options: A) Increase MAP >80 mm Hg || B) Rewarm the patient rapidly || C) Discontinue TTM if STEMI || D) Delay reperfusion until awakening
  - Answer: A · Increase MAP >80 mm Hg · cohort right 24%
  - key_note: DocT keys MAP >80. ERC/ESICM 2021 and AHA 2025 target MAP ≥65 (PCA-17); BOX (2022) found no difference between MAP 63 and 77. With MAP already 65, none of B–D is right either; A is DocT's pick, not the guideline. Cohort 24%.
