## ARDS-11 · ARDS mimics and work-up: — 3 questions

- **[DocT GT · GT2o]** A 55-year-old female patient is intubated for severe respiratory failure. A CT scan is then performed which shows diffuse bilateral ground glass opacities. On arrival in the intensive care unit, the patient desaturates due to abundant hemorrhagic secretions in the endotracheal tube. A fiberoptic bronchoscopy with deep bronchoalveolar lavages is then performed, yielding a very high percentage (>50%) of hemosiderin-laden macrophages in all samples. Which among the following components in this patient's medical history supports the diagnosis?
  - Options: A) Acetylsalicylic acid prophylaxis for carotid artery disease || B) Three spontaneous abortions all happened during the first trimester || C) Polyarteritis nodosa treated with prednisone 60 mg daily || D) Chronic hepatitis B with persistence of circulating HBV DNA
  - Answer: B · Three spontaneous abortions all happened during the first trimester · cohort right 41%
  - line: DocT: BAL with >20% haemosiderin-laden macrophages (here >50%) = diffuse alveolar haemorrhage; then find the systemic cause — ANCA vasculitis, anti-GBM, SLE or antiphospholipid syndrome (clue: three first-trimester miscarriages). PAN spares the lung (medium-vessel) and low-dose aspirin alone does not cause DAH; treat with pulse methylprednisolone ± cyclophosphamide/rituximab and plasma exchange.
- **[DocT GT · GT4]** A patient with hypoxemia and bilateral alveolar infiltrates undergoes bronchoscopy with bronchoalveolar lavage revealing periodic acid-Schiff-positive material. Which condition most likely mimics ARDS?
  - Options: A) Diffuse alveolar haemorrhage || B) Pulmonary alveolar proteinosis || C) Cryptogenic organising pneumonia || D) Acute interstitial pneumonia
  - Answer: B · Pulmonary alveolar proteinosis · cohort right 76%
  - line: DocT: Pulmonary alveolar proteinosis is an ARDS mimic — milky BAL with PAS-positive lipoproteinaceous material, 'crazy paving' on CT — treated by whole-lung lavage (± inhaled GM-CSF), not by ARDS measures. Diffuse alveolar haemorrhage shows haemosiderin-laden macrophages; acute eosinophilic pneumonia shows BAL eosinophils >25%.
  - key_note: Image not needed.
- **[Marrow QBank]** New young smoker, ARDS picture, BAL 35% eosinophils: next step
  - Answer: A · IV methylprednisolone
  - note: ✎ ARDS-11 add: acute eosinophilic pneumonia = young male, recent start of smoking, BAL eos >25%, cultures neg → IV methylprednisolone (response 12–48 h)
  - topic: Acute eosinophilic pneumonia
