## PLEU-13 · True or false? — 1 questions

- **[DocT GT · GT7]** Chest pain caused by diseases of respiratory system usually originates from involvement of?
  - Options: A) Parietal pleura || B) Visceral pleura || C) Pulmonary parenchyma || D) Bronchial airways
  - Answer: A · Parietal pleura · cohort right 50%
  - line: DocT: Respiratory chest pain comes from the parietal pleura (intercostal nerves; central diaphragmatic pleura via the phrenic nerve refers to the shoulder tip). Visceral pleura, lung parenchyma and small airways have no pain fibres, so parenchymal disease hurts only when it reaches the parietal pleura.
