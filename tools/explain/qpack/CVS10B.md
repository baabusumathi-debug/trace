## MI-22 · For posterior and right ventricular MI, give: — 4 questions

- **[DocT QBank]** A 61-year-old male presented with chest tightness, and an ECG was performed, which is shown below. Which arterial territory is most likely to be involved?
  - Options: A) Anteroseptal || B) Anterior || C) Lateral || D) Inferior
  - Answer: D · Inferior · cohort right 77%
  - line: DocT: Inferior STEMI (II, III, aVF): ST elevation III > II with reciprocal ST depression in aVL/I = RCA; II ≥ III with ST elevation in I/aVL or V5–V6 = LCx. The RCA supplies the AV node in ~90%, hence the bradycardia and AV block of inferior MI.
  - key_note: ECG not judged; explanation describes inferior STEMI with III > II and aVL reciprocal change.

## MI-30 · For each cardiac biomarker, give the time to — 3 questions

- **[DocT QBank]** Which cardiac marker begins to rise within 3 to 6 hours of chest pain onset, peaks at 12 to 24 hours and returns to baseline in 7 to 10 days?
  - Options: A) Myoglobin || B) Creatinine kinase (CK) || C) Creatinine kinase-MB (CK-MB) || D) Troponin I
  - Answer: D · Troponin I · cohort right 69%
  - key_note: DocT figures (rise 3–6 h, peak 12–24 h, back in 7–10 days) match troponin I in MI-30; CK-MB normalises by 48–72 h.
- **[DocT GT · GrandT1]** A 58-year-old man presents to the emergency department with chest discomfort for the past 3 hours. ECG is nondiagnostic, and high-sensitivity troponin T (hs-cTnT) levels are within normal limits. The physician is considering additional biomarkers to aid in early rule-in or rule-out of myocardial infarction (MI). Which of the following biomarkers rises earlier than troponin and may improve diagnostic accuracy when used in combination with standard assays?
  - Options: A) Ischemia-modified albumin (IMA) || B) Cardiac myosin-binding protein C (cMyC) || C) B-type natriuretic peptide (BNP) || D) MicroRNA-208b
  - Answer: B · Cardiac myosin-binding protein C (cMyC) · cohort right 57%
  - line: DocT: Cardiac myosin-binding protein C (cMyC) is cardiac-restricted, more abundant than troponin and released faster after injury, so combined with hs-troponin it improves early rule-in/rule-out (comparable accuracy, more patients triaged at presentation). Copeptin is the other early add-on; IMA, BNP and microRNA-208b are not used for MI diagnosis.
- **[Marrow QBank]** Best biomarker for reinfarction on day 3
  - Answer: C · Troponin I
  - note: ✎ MI-30: amend - ACC/ESC use troponin for reinfarction (>20% rise from raised baseline); CK-MB only if troponin unavailable
  - topic: Reinfarction · biomarker
