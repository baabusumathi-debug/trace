- **[DocT GT · PYQ]** Which of the following structures is NOT assessed in a standard FAST scan?
  - Options: A) Heart || B) Liver-kidney interface || C) Brain || D) Spleen-kidney interface
  - Answer: C · Brain
  - line: DocT: FAST has four windows for free fluid: pericardial (subxiphoid), RUQ (Morison's pouch), LUQ (splenorenal recess) and pelvis (pouch of Douglas or rectovesical pouch). eFAST adds both anterior chests for pneumothorax. It does not image the brain or the retroperitoneum.

## POLY-09 · ATLS classes of haemorrhagic shock: — 4 questions

- **[DocT GT · GT3]** A postpartum patient with ongoing vaginal bleeding receives a 1-liter fluid bolus. Her blood pressure initially improves but drops again within 15 minutes, and her heart rate increases. She is alert but diaphoretic. What is the most appropriate next step?
  - Options: A) No further intervention needed || B) Begin vasopressors only || C) Continue fluids, consider transfusion || D) Wait and observe for stability
  - Answer: C · Continue fluids, consider transfusion · cohort right 93%
  - line: DocT: Response to a fluid bolus (ATLS): rapid responder = stable, <20% blood loss; transient responder = improves then deteriorates, meaning ongoing bleeding with ~20-40% loss → blood products, uterotonics/MTP and source control; non-responder = >40% loss or a non-haemorrhagic cause → immediate transfusion and surgery. Vasopressors do not replace volume.
