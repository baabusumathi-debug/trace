## PCA-47 · For biomarkers in — 2 questions

- **[DocT GT · GT5]** An amateur boxer shows elevated CSF neurofilament light chain (NF-L) after bouts. What does this finding most likely suggest?
  - Options: A) BBB closure || B) Axonal injury || C) Glial scarring || D) Synaptic sprouting
  - Answer: B · Axonal injury · cohort right 69%
  - line: DocT: Neurofilament light chain (NfL) is a structural protein of large myelinated AXONS, so a raised CSF/serum NfL means axonal injury — it rises even after repetitive mild TBI (boxers) and is the 2025 post-arrest biomarker. Contrast: NSE = neuronal cell body, S-100B/GFAP = glia/astrocytes, so 'glial scarring' and 'BBB closure' are wrong.
- **[DocT GT · GT5]** A biomarker linked with neuronal injury and known for false-positive results due to hemolysis is most likely which of the following?
  - Options: A) NSE || B) UCH-L1 || C) S100B || D) NF-H
  - Answer: A · NSE · cohort right 71%
  - line: DocT: NSE is also present in red cells and platelets, so a HAEMOLYSED sample (or haemolysis on ECMO) gives a falsely high NSE — check free Hb before using it to prognosticate. S100B (glia), UCH-L1 (neuronal, more brain-specific) and NF-H are not raised by haemolysis in the same way.

- **[DocT GT · GT6]** On what day is an MRI Brain advised in a post-cardiac arrest patient?
  - Options: A) Day 5-7 || B) Immediately after cardiac arrest || C) Days 2-5 || D) Days 3-7
  - Answer: D · Days 3-7 · cohort right 69%
  - line: DocT: Keys MRI at days 3-7 after arrest. ERC-ESICM 2021/2025 places DWI-MRI at 2-5 days after ROSC in the multimodal algorithm (AHA 2023: 2-7 days); the window starts after TTM and sedation clearance because early DWI underestimates injury and later the ADC changes pseudo-normalise.
