## ALF-16 · Why does the brain swell in acute — 1 questions

- **[DocT GT · CGT2]** The edema surrounding brain abscess is predominantly:
  - Options: A) Cytotoxic || B) Vasogenic || C) Interstitial || D) Osmotic
  - Answer: B · Vasogenic
  - line: DocT: The oedema around a brain abscess (or tumour) is vasogenic: the blood-brain barrier breaks down and protein-rich fluid leaks into the white-matter extracellular space, so it responds to steroids. Cytotoxic oedema (cells swell, BBB intact) is ischaemic, as in stroke and the astrocyte swelling of ALF, and does not respond to steroids.

## ALF-26 · Infection in acute liver failure: — 1 questions

- **[DocT GT · CGT1]** A 33-year-old ALF patient on day 5 develops fever, rising WBC, new infiltrates on CXR. Which is best empiric step?
  - Options: A) Narrow-spectrum penicillin || B) Extended-spectrum β -lactam || C) High-dose amphotericin || D) Hold antibiotics until cultures
  - Answer: B · Extended-spectrum β -lactam
  - line: DocT: Suspected infection in ALF (pneumonia is commonest; fever and leucocytosis may be absent) needs immediate broad empirical cover with an extended-spectrum beta-lactam (piperacillin-tazobactam or a carbapenem, guided by local Gram-negative ecology). Add antifungals only for deterioration despite antibiotics or a prolonged course, and never hold antibiotics for cultures.

## ALF-28 · Circulation, kidney, bleeding and — 1 questions

- **[DocT GT · CGT1]** A patient with ALF develops persistent hypotension despite norepinephrine. Which next step is recommended?
  - Options: A) High-dose corticosteroids || B) Low-dose hydrocortisone || C) High-dose vasopressin || D) Epinephrine bolus
  - Answer: B · Low-dose hydrocortisone
  - line: DocT: ALF causes hyperdynamic vasodilatory shock with frequent relative adrenal insufficiency. Use noradrenaline first, vasopressin or terlipressin as adjunct, and low-dose hydrocortisone (200 mg/day) for vasopressor-refractory shock; it lowers the vasopressor dose (AASLD/EASL) without proven survival benefit. High-dose steroids and adrenaline boluses are wrong.
