## Final FLAGS
- REC-01 c: "In children: inhalants" — inhalants are the only class used more by children than adults (1.17% vs 0.58%), not the commonest substance in children. Reword.
- REC-03 c: "maximum of 2 mg" — not a true ceiling; up to about 10 mg before excluding opioid toxicity.
- REC-09 a: "detected as a benzodiazepine on screening" — often missed by routine immunoassay; and "4–6 hours" is short (effects 8–12 h or more).
- REC-13 c: "2 mg per dose to a maximum of 12 mg" — wrong; standard is 12 mg, then 2 mg 2-hourly, maintenance 8 mg 6-hourly (12–32 mg/day).
- REC-15 watch: "1 degree every 10 minutes" — standard is about 1 °C (up to 2 °C) every 5 minutes; "all inhaled agents except N2O" → "all volatile agents"; onset up to 24 h is generous.
