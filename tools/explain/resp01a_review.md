# RESP01A review — independent fact-check (6 Nov)

## Changes
- ARDS-03 trap: "22% chose this" (pulmonary oedema) — the qpack gives only 78% right, not the split by option → "About a fifth of the cohort missed this, and pulmonary oedema is the obvious pull". Source: qpack GT7.
- ARDS-03 mimic: "AFE is not a criterion-based diagnosis" was wrong (diagnostic criteria exist, e.g. SMFM/Clark 2016) → AFE described as sudden cardiorespiratory collapse in labour or just after delivery, with hypotension and DIC, unlike this vignette.
- ARDS-04 c: "NIV delays intubation and raises mortality" stated as fact → NIV often fails, and delayed intubation is associated with higher mortality (LUNG SAFE NIV analysis, Bellani AJRCCM 2017: higher ICU mortality with NIV when P/F <150). HACOR >5 at 1 h checked (Duan, ICM 2017) and kept.
- ARDS-04 trap: "(63% of the cohort)" implied 63% chose compensated respiratory acidosis → "only 37% of the cohort got this right". ABG checked by recalculation: pH 7.295 / PaCO₂ 63 / HCO₃⁻ 29.8 is internally consistent and is a partly compensated respiratory acidosis; P/F 58/0.75 = 77.
- ARDS-05 trap/decode: the trap treated Marrow's KEYED answer (PCWP 16 mmHg) as the wrong belief → trap now "A wedge above 18 rules out ARDS" (AECC thinking); a low wedge still favours non-cardiogenic oedema, so pick the key when offered. Decode reworded to match. Source: Berlin 2012 (JAMA); AECC 1994.
- ARDS-07 b: "commonest surgical causes" → "commonest causes in trauma" (lung contusion, multiple fractures, chest-wall trauma). APACHE ≥16 = 2.5-fold risk in trauma verified. Source: Harrison's ARDS chapter.
- ARDS-09 traps: "(half the cohort)" → "only half the cohort chose shunt"; added that shunt is the extreme of V/Q mismatch (V/Q = 0), which is why the AIM2 key is defensible.
- ARDS-11 table: plasma exchange given as routine for DAH → "plasma exchange for anti-GBM disease and selected vasculitis" (after PEXIVAS, not routine in ANCA vasculitis). Source: PEXIVAS NEJM 2020; KDIGO 2024 ANCA vasculitis.
- ARDS-12 a: added Gattinoni's L/H types (L: low elastance, low lung weight, poorly recruitable; H: high elastance, heavy lung, shunt, recruitable) and the evidence against them (Grasselli, Lancet Respir Med 2020; Ferrando, ICM 2020: compliance spread over the classical ARDS range with no separate groups; no trial of L/H-guided ventilation). New trap (larger tidal volumes for type L) and a [pattern] decode item.
- ARDS-12 c: added that the RECOVERY dose was 6 mg once daily for up to 10 days (or until discharge). Source: RECOVERY, NEJM 2021.
- FLAGS ARDS-05: "the key is outdated" was too strong → the key rests on AECC but is still acceptable as the best option; it is not a Berlin criterion.

Checked and left unchanged (correct): Ashbaugh 1967, 12 patients, Lancet; AECC 1994 (acute, P/F ≤300 ALI / ≤200 ARDS regardless of PEEP, PAOP ≤18 or no left atrial hypertension, bilateral infiltrates on frontal CXR); Berlin 2012 (1 week; bilateral opacities on CXR/CT; not fully explained by cardiac failure/fluid overload, echo if no risk factor; P/F ≤300 on PEEP/CPAP ≥5; CPAP allowed only in mild; mortality 27/32/45% in the validation cohort); 2023 global definition (HFNO ≥30 L/min or NIV/CPAP ≥5; S/F ≤315 with SpO₂ ≤97%; S/F 315/235/148 for P/F 300/200/100; lung ultrasound by trained operators; no PEEP, flow or device requirement in resource-limited settings, after Kigali/Riviello 2016; non-intubated patients not graded); LUNG SAFE (459 ICUs, 50 countries, 29,144 patients, 10.4% of admissions, 23.4% of ventilated, 30.0/46.6/23.4%, recognition 60.2% overall and 51.3% in mild, prone 16.3% in severe, hospital mortality ~40% rising with grade 34.9/40.3/46.1%, pneumonia the leading risk factor in well over half); direct vs indirect lists; stage timings (card's notes vs Harrison's 7–21 d) flagged in the text; Gattinoni baby lung and the sponge lung; shunt vs V/Q; dead-space fraction as an independent mortality predictor (Nuckton NEJM 2002); DAH BAL >20% haemosiderin-laden macrophages; AEP BAL eosinophils >25%; PAP milky PAS-positive lavage, whole-lung lavage ± inhaled GM-CSF; hyperinflammatory phenotype (Calfee 2014).

## Final FLAGS
- ARDS-04: WATCH "PEEP below 5 and not on HFNO: cannot be graded by Berlin" mixes two definitions — Berlin never accepts HFNO; HFNO ≥30 L/min counts only under the 2023 definition, which does not grade non-intubated patients. DocT NIV key calls P/F 200 "mild" (it is moderate); DocT's ABG explanation ("no acid–base disturbance") is wrong — partly compensated respiratory acidosis; the key (severe hypoxaemia, P/F 77) stands.
- ARDS-05: Marrow key PCWP 16 mmHg rests on the AECC wedge rule; acceptable as the best option (a low wedge favours non-cardiogenic oedema), but not a Berlin criterion, and a raised PAOP can coexist with ARDS. Card is right.
- ARDS-07: Card puts transfusion (TRALI) in the DIRECT column; standard sources (Ware & Matthay NEJM 2000; the Berlin risk-factor list) class transfusion/TRALI as indirect.
- ARDS-09: DocT AIM2 keys V/Q mismatch for ARDS hypoxaemia; correct mechanism is shunt; the key is defensible only because shunt is not offered.
