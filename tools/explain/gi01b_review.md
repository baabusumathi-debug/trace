## Settled from the writer's open items
- Vaquero ICP-monitor bleeding: **10.3%** confirmed (58 surveyed monitored patients, ALFSG, Liver Transpl 2005; about half incidental). Karvellas 2014: 7%.
- Hypoglycaemia frequency (40%): **not traced**. EASL says it is common, with checks every 2 h in hyperacute ALF. Left as the card's figure.
- Commonest cause of death: **India (AIIMS series):** cerebral oedema, with 82% vs 44% mortality, 58% oedema at admission and two-thirds of deaths within 72 h (Acharya 2000). **West:** multiorgan failure and severe sepsis (EASL 2017; Jalan-group review: "sepsis has emerged as the most common cause of death").
- ACG 2023: ICP-monitoring wording confirmed ("no conclusive evidence to recommend routine ICP monitor placement"). The sodium target wording (145–150, <160) is **not verified**; the full text could not be reached.
- Anti-TB thresholds: ATS 2006 confirmed. Stop at ALT >5× ULN, or >3× with jaundice and/or symptoms. Restart at ALT <2× ULN with rifampicin ± ethambutol, then isoniazid after 3–7 days; if ALT rises again, stop the last drug added; pyrazinamide rechallenge is hazardous after prolonged or severe injury. Sharma 2010 confirmed: 19/175 recurrences, 13.8/10.2/8.6%, p = 0.69.
- Also confirmed: SCCM 2023 Part II items, EASL figures (infection 60–80%, pneumonia 50%, fungal about one-third, PaCO2 4.5–5.5 kPa, hyperventilation 25–30, indomethacin 0.5 mg/kg, jugular bulb 55/80%, mannitol 150 mL 20%, hypertonic saline 200 mL 2.7% or 20 mL 30%, CPP >50, vasopressin 1–2 U/h above NA 0.2–0.3), LOLA trial (42.4% vs 33.3%).
