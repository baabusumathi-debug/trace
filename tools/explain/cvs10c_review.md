## Changes

1. 2. MI-52 d (factual): "prasugrel and ticagrelor were not tested with lysis" → ticagrelor was tested starting a median of about 11 h after lysis in patients <75 y (TREAT: TIMI major bleeding 0.73 vs 0.69%, non-inferior); prasugrel untested (ACC TREAT summary).

## Final FLAGS
- MI-38: b is incomplete. Riociguat is also a nitrate contraindication, and ESC adds marked bradycardia. The DocT oesophageal-spasm key is debatable.
- MI-39: d. ESC's indication is SBP >120 mmHg with no acute HF (IIa A), not tachycardia. The contraindications are also wider than HF and hypotension: heart block (PR >240 ms or 2nd/3rd degree), bronchospasm, and high shock risk (COMMIT).
- MI-45: c. The trial attribution is loose: MATRIX also lowered mortality, RIVAL's overall result was neutral, and RIFLE-STEACS lowered cardiac mortality and shortened stay.
- MI-53: the card is correct for the ESC list. The windows differ from ACC/AHA (ischaemic stroke <3 months; major surgery <3 weeks is relative) and from ESC PE (surgery <3 weeks). The DocT GT AVM key is explained in the trap.
