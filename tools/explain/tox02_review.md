# TOX02 review — independent fact-check of tox02_learn.py

## Changes
- APAP-09: no part 'a' (IV 21-hour protocol was only in the intro) → added ('a', bold three-bag protocol + why, infusion rates 12.5/6.25 mg/kg/h, 15→60 min history); intro rewritten to set the scene without the answer; table kept. (Card answer; Kerr 2005 for 15 vs 60 min.)
- APAP-09 b: "course is longer because vomiting makes absorption unreliable" (wrong reason) → low oral bioavailability (~10%) and taste/vomiting; 72 h length is from the original US protocol design. (Goldfrank's.)
- APAP-09: added extra on newer regimens → two-bag 200 mg/kg/4 h + 100 mg/kg/16 h; SNAP 100 mg/kg/2 h + 200 mg/kg/10 h; SNAP cut vomiting and reactions, two-bag mainly reactions. (Bateman, Lancet 2014; UIC drug-info review 2025; RCH guideline.)
- APAP-12 b: dialysis had no criteria → EXTRIP 2014 criteria added: level >1000 mg/L without NAC; mitochondrial dysfunction (altered mental status, acidosis, raised lactate) with >700 mg/L without NAC or >900 mg/L with NAC; intermittent HD preferred; raise NAC rate. (Gosselin et al., Clin Toxicol 2014.)
- APAP-12 decode: "comatose, lactic acidosis at 6 h → HD" (no level) → adds level 950 mg/L on NAC, HD per EXTRIP plus increased NAC. (EXTRIP 2014.)
- APAP-12 a: fomepizole → adds mouse data on JNK/mitochondrial protection; human evidence case reports only, no guideline dose/threshold. (Akakpo et al., Hepatology 2018.)
- APAP-11 c: lactate timing only restated card's "1 hour" → body now gives Bernal 2002 original: mmol/L, ~4 h after admission (>3.5) and ~12 h after full resuscitation (>3.0); sensitivity of King's 76% → 91%. Table unit added. (Bernal, Lancet 2002.)
- APAP-08 c: "paracetamol is accepted as an exception to the 1-hour window" (overstated) → usual window 1 h; some guidelines extend (ANZ: 2 h for toxic IR dose, up to 4 h for >30 g or modified-release). (ANZ guideline summary, MJA 2015.)
- APAP-04 c: "any measurable paracetamol beyond trace" (wrong; the trigger is >20) → level >20 µg/mL means enough drug to keep making NAPQI; added guideline range of dose thresholds. (ANZ / LITFL; Dart 2006.)
- APAP-06 intro/a: "level falls with half-life of about 4 h" → line is drawn with a 4-h half-life on purpose; the drug's own is 2–3 h. (Goldfrank's.)
- APAP-06 d: "review prompted by deaths below the old lines" (unverified) → Commission on Human Medicines review 2012; MHRA single 100 line; more patients treated. (MHRA 2012.)
- APAP-06 mimic: "a level in mg/dL is a misprint" (wrong) → unit conversion: mg/L = µg/mL; µmol/L ≈ ×6.6 (150 ≈ 990 µmol/L; 100 ≈ 660 µmol/L).
- APAP-06 extra: "over 200 mg/kg, level unavailable → don't wait" (wrong: only if no level before 8 h) → over 150 mg/kg and level not back before 8 h → start NAC. (APAP-14 card; Tintinalli.)
- APAP-05 d: added that stage IV runs ~day 4 to 2 weeks, histology up to 3 months (minor; bold kept to card). (Goldfrank's.)
- APAP-14 trap: "send a level in analgesic co-ingestion with GI symptoms" → send a paracetamol level in every deliberate overdose (standard practice).
- APAP-02 b: "other CYP450 isoforms" → names CYP3A4 and CYP1A2 (minor).
- APAP-03 d: Gilbert "reduces UGT1A1 glucuronidation" stated as fact → theoretical, clinical evidence weak (minor).
- APAP-08 trap: reworded "Wait for the 4-hour level before giving NAC at 7 hours" for clarity (minor).
- Checked and left as is: Rumack–Matthew 200/150 lines and UK 100→15 at 15 h; King's criteria (pH <7.30; INR >6.5 + creatinine >3.4 mg/dL + grade 3–4 HE), sensitivity ~60%/specificity ~90% (Craig 2010 meta-analysis); adduct cut-off >1 nmol/mL with ALT >1000 (James 2009, 1.1 nmol/mL); AcetaSTAT; NAC stopping criteria and 12-hourly bloods (ANZ 2015); oral 140 + 70 × 17.

## Final FLAGS
- APAP-01: WATCH says pesticides "dominate" — true of poisoning, but acute liver failure in India is mostly viral hepatitis (HEV, HBV) and anti-TB drugs.
- APAP-03: card calls anticonvulsants "CYP2E1 inducers"; only isoniazid (and alcohol) act mainly on CYP2E1; evidence for inducers is weak.
- APAP-04: card's 7.5 g / 4 g in 24 h repeated-ingestion threshold matches no guideline exactly (Dart 2006 / ANZ: ≥10 g or 200 mg/kg in 24 h; ≥6 g or 150 mg/kg/24 h for ≥48 h; ANZ 4 g or 100 mg/kg with risk factors). Level >20 and ALT >50 triggers agree. (Writer's flag corrected: the figures it called "US consensus" are the 2006 out-of-hospital consensus, shared with ANZ.)
- APAP-05: "recovery usually complete by day 7" is optimistic (Goldfrank: day 4–5 to 2 weeks; histology up to 3 months).
- APAP-08 (new): charcoal "within 4 hours" is broader than most guidelines (1 h usual; ANZ 2 h, 4 h only for >30 g or modified-release).
- APAP-11: lactate timing "after 1 hour" does not match Bernal 2002 (~4 h after admission >3.5; ~12 h after full resuscitation >3.0 mmol/L).
- APAP-14 (new): WATCH "12 hours of IV NAC then repeat tests" for repeated ingestion is source-specific, not universal.
