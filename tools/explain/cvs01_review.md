## Settled points (writer's open questions)
- **ESC 2025 diagnosis:** a compatible presentation plus additional criteria. More than one additional criterion makes it definite and one makes it possible. The additional criteria are rub, ECG, raised CRP, new or worse effusion, and CMR oedema/LGE. Correct as written.
- **ESC 2025 effusion size:** large is >20 mm. Correct.
- **ESC 2025 doses:** aspirin 750–1000 mg tds, ibuprofen 600–800 mg tds, colchicine 0.5 mg bd (od if <70 kg or severe renal impairment), prednisone 0.2–0.5 mg/kg/day. All correct.
- **Steroids as initial therapy:** class III per the Imazio review. The recommendation tables could not be read in the OUP text.
- **IL-1 blockers:** see CVS-18 a. Summaries disagree on class I vs IIa (most say class I). Kept as "reported as class I".
- **Mercé, Gillam, Himelman, IMPI:** confirmed (see above).
- **Disputed keys kept as traps; the "truth" side checks out:**

  - CVS-24/26: RV collapse is keyed as the most specific sign, though Gillam's prolonged RA collapse was 100% specific.
  - CVS-04: viral, not uraemic.
