# CVS10D review (second, independent check), 19 Nov 2026

Sources read: ESC 2023 ACS full text (Byrne et al., Eur Heart J 2023, recommendation tables 9, 10, 14 and 16); ESC 2022 VA guideline section 7.1.1 body text (academic.oup.com); CardioNerds and ACC summaries of the ACC/AHA 2025 ACS guideline; DanGer Shock (NEJM 2024, via Wiki Journal Club and ACC); ESC Congress 2025 Hot Lines (BETAMI-DANBLOCK and the LVEF 40–49% meta-analysis); CNIC release on REBOOT; Arnaoutakis 2012 (STS VSR registry, PubMed). Confirmed as written: ACEi I A / IIa A; beta-blocker I A / IIa B; repeat LVEF at 6–12 weeks (I C); ECG monitoring ≥24 h (I C); IRA-only PCI in shock (I B); routine IABP III B; short-term MCS IIb C; IABP for mechanical complications IIa C; LV thrombus OAC 3–6 months (IIa C); the three bradycardia rows (I C); IABP-SHOCK II and ECLS-SHOCK numbers; DanGer Shock HR 0.74 (0.55–0.99), NNT 8, composite RR 4.74; ACC/AHA 2025 microaxial pump 2a, routine IABP and VA-ECMO class 3: No Benefit; GRACE in-hospital model (8 variables) vs post-discharge model (9 variables); TIMI event rates. All traps on disputed DocT keys (MI-60 CGT4 and GT6, MI-64 'next' vs 'definitive') were kept; their 'truth' sides check out.

## Changes
- Header: added a second-check block listing what was verified and where.
- MI-57 c (factual): the explanation did not say that ESC 2023 grades MRA **I, A**. It now says so, after the card's 'I, B' (ESC 2023 Table 16).
- MI-57 a (minor): BETAMI-DANBLOCK result 'fewer events' → 'HR 0.85, mainly fewer reinfarctions, with no difference in death' (ESC Hot Line 2025).
- MI-58 b (minor): 'magnesium above about 1 mmol/L' (a figure above the normal range) → 'potassium at 4 mmol/L or above and magnesium in the high-normal range'.
- MI-59 a and c (factual): added that ESC 2023 merges the amiodarone row into 'IV beta-blocker and/or amiodarone for polymorphic VT/VF' (I, B). This sits alongside the ESC 2022 VA wording (IIa, 'should be considered'). Added amiodarone's mechanism (c had no 'why').
- MI-59 f and trap (factual): 'prophylactic lidocaine raised mortality' → 'did not reduce, and tended to increase, mortality'. The old meta-analyses showed only a non-significant trend. Also 'class I drugs (CAST)' → 'class IC drugs given for post-MI ectopy'.
- MI-60 c (depth): added ESC 2023 Table 14: a permanent pacemaker only if high-degree block persists ≥5 days (I, C), and no pacing once it has resolved (III, B).
- MI-63 c (factual): the DanGer Shock inclusion criteria 'lactate >2.5 and SBP <100' → 'LVEF <45%, lactate ≥2.5 mmol/L, SBP <100 mmHg or a vasopressor'. The exclusions are now listed: GCS <8 after OHCA, severe RV failure, mechanical complications. Added '360 randomised, 355 analysed'.
- MI-63 d (factual): the explanation implied that RRT was part of the safety composite. It now lists the real composite: severe bleeding, limb ischaemia, haemolysis, device failure and worsening AR, 24.0% vs 6.2%. RRT is given as a separate outcome, 41.9% vs 26.7%.
- MI-63 e (minor): ACC/AHA 2025 pump recommendation specified as STEMI with severe or refractory shock, 2a.
- MI-64 c (minor): added ESC 2023 IABP IIa, C and ACC/AHA 2025 short-term MCS as a bridge for mechanical complications, 2a B-NR (CardioNerds).
- MI-64 d and MI-65 b (factual): the card's 'early surgery 20–40%' is now stated as optimistic. STS registry operative mortality was 54.1% for repair within 7 days and 18.4% after (Arnaoutakis 2012).
- MI-66 extra (minor): the Q-wave definition now adds the Fourth Universal Definition thresholds: ≥30 ms and ≥1 mm deep in two contiguous leads, and in V2–V3 any Q over 20 ms or a QS complex.
- FLAGS: MI-57, MI-59 and MI-63 rewritten; MI-64 added; MI-65 extended.

## Final FLAGS
- MI-56: c: the repeat LVEF is at 6–12 weeks (ESC 2023 I, C), not the notes' 3–6 weeks. The watch line is right.
- MI-57: c: MRA 'I, B' is the ESC 2017 level; ESC 2023 gives I, A. The ACE inhibitor and beta-blocker classes are confirmed. Beta-blocker for LVEF >40% is contested.
- MI-59: c: 'IV amiodarone I, C' is the 2017 wording. ESC 2023 has 'IV beta-blocker and/or amiodarone' at I, B; ESC 2022 VA has IIa. f: 'prophylactic III, B' is also 2017 wording.
- MI-60: the proximal/distal split is simplified, since inferior MI also causes nodal complete block. Both DocT GT keys are faulty (CGT4 wrong key; GT6 has two true options).
- MI-62: e: the IIb list (PA catheter, ultrafiltration, inotropes) is ESC 2017. ESC 2023 keeps MCS at IIb, C; ACC/AHA 2025 gives the microaxial pump 2a.
- MI-63: d: RRT is not part of the safety composite (that was 24.0 vs 6.2%; RRT 41.9 vs 26.7%). e: ACC/AHA 2025 restricts the pump to STEMI.
- MI-64: VSR 'early surgery 20–40%' understates it (STS 54% within 7 days).
- MI-65: a: 'posterolateral' should read inferior. b: 20–40% is low.
- MI-66: c: 'long-term OAC' is stronger than ESC 2023, which gives 3–6 months guided by imaging (IIa, C).
- MI-67: the nine variables belong to the post-discharge 6-month model; the >140 cut-off comes from the in-hospital model.
