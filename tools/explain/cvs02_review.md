## Changes
- CVS-31b (factual): the 1–5% athlete figure was given without context → adds that athlete reviews quote about 5–20% (Compagnucci, Medicina 2021; ACC 2018 gives 5–22% in the young) and the UK registry found about 1% of all community SCD (Bhatia, Circ Arrhythm Electrophysiol 2023); exercise restriction worded as ESC 2025 “initially 1 month” (Rev Esp Cardiol insights).
- CVS-31c (minor): 8.6% was credited to Fabre and Sheppard, which I could not confirm → now “autopsy figure quoted in Schultz, Mayo Clin Proc 2009”, plus the ESC 2013 range of 2–42% of young SCD (ESC 2013 position statement).
- CVS-31d (factual): no guideline anchor → adds ESC 2013: biopsy-proven myocarditis in 9–16% of adult unexplained non-ischaemic DCM, 46% of children with an identified cause; higher figures come from immunohistochemistry or viral genomes.
- CVS-31e (factual): 79%/56% was presented as a general figure and tied to MTT → now credited to Magnani, Am Heart J 2006 (112 consecutive biopsy-proven patients); the MTT 20%/56% mortality is kept as a similar cohort.
- CVS-29b (minor): “the rest must be lymphocytes” → the monocyte cap stops resident macrophages alone from meeting the count, so the infiltrate is mostly lymphocytic.
- CVS-33c (factual): called the whole list “virus-negative immune forms” where immunosuppression has a role → now only GCM, sarcoid and vasculitis/CTD forms; thyrotoxicosis and diabetes are associations, not indications.
- CVS-34b (minor): “4.4%, more than in controls” was unverified → now the Matsumori 1998 Japanese multicentre figures (HCV antibody 10.6% HCM, 6.3% DCM, 2.4% blood donors).
- CVS-34d/e (depth): India part had no explanation; Kandolf part needed the caveat → Indian data are small series, and low-level genomes (especially PVB19) occur in normal hearts.
- CVS-36e (minor): troponin “may peak later or stay elevated longer than an infarct” was unverified → removed; now says troponin height does not separate myocarditis from MI.
- CVS-38a + punch (factual): fulminant myocarditis “often recovers well” rested only on McCarthy 2000 → adds Ammirati (Circulation 2017/2019): more death and transplant than non-fulminant, but function often recovers in survivors who are supported.
- CVS-43 trap + decode, CVS-45 trap + decode (factual, trap truth side): said AHA excludes “no EF improvement after optimal therapy”, so Marrow’s key follows AHA → AHA/ACC/ESC 2007 lists failure to respond to usual care within 1–2 weeks as Class I (2 weeks–3 months, dilated LV) or IIa (>3 months), and ESC 2025 lists failure to respond; the Marrow key is disputed under both; exam day: pick it only in Marrow’s question (PMC5295512 table of the 2007 statement; Rev Esp Cardiol ESC 2025 insights).
- CVS-44d (minor): “ESC 2025 grades unlikely/possible/definite” was not confirmed → “definite when a compatible presentation is confirmed by CMR or EMB; lower-certainty findings possible” (Rev Esp Cardiol; MDC editorial).
- CVS-46e + punch (factual): implied that transplant at about 4 weeks is guideline practice → ESC 2013: defer transplant in the acute phase, consider it when support cannot be weaned; ESC 2025: refractory cases need long-term MCS or transplant; the 4 weeks is a rule of thumb.
- CVS-46 Marrow decode (minor): “keyed LVAD” left as is → adds that the first device is temporary (VA-ECMO ± microaxial pump), and a durable LVAD comes only if there is no recovery.
- CVS-47b (factual): “ESC 2025 strong recommendations for giant-cell and eosinophilic” could not be confirmed → ESC 2025 says not as initial therapy; reserve for biopsy-proven or highly suspected immune forms; IIa C fulminant non-infectious; IIb C refractory (Rev Esp Cardiol; esanum).
- CVS-47c (minor): “this is why MTT failed and TIMIC worked” overstated → “patient selection is the likely reason”.
- CVS-48d (minor): now quotes ESC 2013 (“ineffective in IMAC … we do not give recommendations”); ESC 2025 summaries do not mention IVIG.
- Header: added the second-review source list and what is still unverified.

Checked and correct as written: ESC 2013 clinical-suspicion rule (≥1 + ≥1 from different categories; ≥2 if asymptomatic; no CAD ≥50%, no other cause), the 14/4/7 immunohistochemical counts, MTT (111 patients, LVEF <45%, 24 weeks), TIMIC, IMAC 2 g/kg, Lake Louise 2009/2018, ESC 2025 EMB, CMR, serology, NSAID/colchicine and MCS statements, AHA 2007 EMB scenarios in CVS-43b, the DocT CVS-42 trap (LGE extent is prognostic).

## Final FLAGS
- CVS-29: the 14/4/7 counts are the ESC 2013 immunohistochemical criteria, not Dallas; c is ≥7 CD3+ cells/mm².
- CVS-31 (new): athlete 1–5% at the low end (reviews 5–20%; ESC 2013 2–42% of young SCD); DCM share is 9–16% in ESC 2013, against the card’s 10–40%; 79%/56% is Magnani 2006 (biopsy-proven cohort).
- CVS-42: card follows Lake Louise 2009; ESC 2025 uses 2018 (one T2 plus one T1 criterion).
- CVS-43 (new): the card-layer cross-note is wrong to say AHA excludes failure to improve; AHA 2007 makes it Class I/IIa, so the Marrow key is disputed under both AHA and ESC.
- CVS-44: card’s “>1/>2” should be “≥1/≥2”, and the ≥2 rule applies only if asymptomatic.
- CVS-46 (new): “transplant if no recovery within 4 weeks” is not a guideline cut-off.
