# HY5 review (independent fact-check, 22 Nov 2026)

Checked all 19 cards in hy5_learn.py against cards/HY5.jsonl, qpack/HY5.md and endo_cardlayer.json. There are no Top-up cards. `check_learn.py HY5` gives OK (19/19, spine of 6).

## Changes (card: what was wrong → what it says now, with the source)

Factual
- HEO-03 c and trap: said the "8 hours" is only the length of the second infusion, "not a time window" → now says CRASH-2 enrolled patients up to 8 h after injury. All-cause death was 16.0 vs 14.5%, bleeding death 5.7 vs 4.9%. The benefit was confined to the first 3 h and later TXA increased bleeding deaths, so 3 h is the treatment window. (CRASH-2 Lancet 2010 abstract; 2011 timing analysis)
- HEO-14 trap and punch: said "ACOG/SMFM 2021: 36+0–37+6, or 34+0–35+6 with recurrent bleeding" → ACOG CO 831 gives 36+0–37+6 for praevia. Its 34+0–35+6 window is for accreta, and it has no bleeding modifier. RCOG GTG 27a (2018) gives 34+0–36+6 for praevia with bleeding. The verdict (caesarean at 36 weeks) is unchanged. (ACOG CO 831 PDF on the SMFM site; NHS Scotland summary of RCOG GTG 27a)
- HEO-17 c and punch: said "ectopic was not part of [ACOG 2024's] relaxation, and NICE gives anti-D after surgical management of an ectopic". Neither claim holds up → NICE NG126 (current text) says do not offer anti-D for ectopic pregnancy or miscarriage up to 11+6 weeks. ACOG 2024 lets anti-D be forgone after abortion or pregnancy loss before 12 weeks, and its summaries do not mention ectopic pregnancy. The card's 50 µg is kept as the traditional ACOG PB 181 answer and the exam-day answer. (NICE NG126 1.18.1–1.18.3; PubMed 39255498; UMEM, ebmedicine and ASRM summaries)
- HEO-07 b: said "JTA 2016 prefers methimazole" → JTA accepts either drug and finds methimazole no worse. ATA 2016 prefers PTU in storm; doses added. (JTA 2016 full text, Recommendation 2; ATA 2016 Table 7)
- HEO-04 c: said "about a fifth of TACO reports have fever", a figure I could not find → now says fever does not exclude TACO, so the card's "Fever: No" describes the usual case only. (SHOT TACO chapter)
- Header: the ISBT TACO definition was given as "respiratory compromise plus ≥2 of …" → now says A and/or B plus a total of ≥3 of the five criteria A–E. The HEO-04 a explanation now states this as well. (ISBT 2018 via SHOT)

Minor (depth, wording, softened specifics)
- HEO-07 a, c, d: added where JTA 2016 differs from ATA 2016. JTA prefers β1-selective blockers (landiolol, esmolol, bisoprolol) and does not recommend propranolol for the tachycardia; it gives iodide at the same time as the thionamide. Added the ATA doses: propranolol 60–80 mg 4-hourly, SSKI 5 drops 6-hourly no earlier than 1 h after the thionamide, hydrocortisone 300 mg then 100 mg 8-hourly. The exam answers still follow ATA. (JTA Recommendations 3, 4, 8; ATA Table 7)
- HEO-08 c: added that the 2024 consensus cuts insulin to 0.05 U/kg/h when dextrose starts. HEO-08 e: added that the 2024 consensus says not to use the anion gap for resolution. (ADA/EASD 2024 consensus, Diabetologia full text)
- HEO-05 d: "HELLP does not occur before 20 weeks" → "almost never".
- HEO-05 e: "pegfilgrastim lasts about two weeks" was a loose mechanism → now says it is cleared by neutrophils and keeps acting until the count recovers, which is why the label rules out the 14 days before chemotherapy. (Neulasta label)

## Final FLAGS
- HEO-08: a "K below 3.3" is the ADA 2009 figure; the 2024 consensus delays insulin until K is above 3.5. d: 2024 gives bicarbonate only below pH 7.0. e: 2024 resolution is ketones <0.6 with pH ≥7.3 or bicarbonate ≥18, and the anion gap is no longer used.
- HEO-04: the table's "TACO within 6–12 h" and "Fever: No" should read: onset up to 12 h, ≥3 ISBT criteria, fever does not exclude TACO. Card layer: the GrandT1 Q43 type II key is wrong (type I is right); the AIM2 Q105 TRALI key needs an assumed transfusion.
- HEO-11: wording only. ACOG gives the load over 20–30 min and 2–4 g over 5 min for a recurrent seizure. The DocT "magnesium first" key with pulmonary oedema is the least-wrong single answer.
- HEO-02: the card agrees with AABB 2023 and AABB/ICTMG 2025. The neurosurgery figure of 100 is BSH. The GT7 6 g/dL key is the old ASA wording. The GT8 aplastic-anaemia prophylaxis key rests on the stem's "chemotherapy".
- HEO-03: card correct. DocT's "within 8 hours" is CRASH-2's enrolment window; the answer is 3 h. In the GT1o ratio question options B and C are printed identically.
- HEO-13: the card folds part d into line c. Tennessee is ≥600 / ≥70 / ≤100. ACOG still allows a 48-h steroid delay for stable HELLP before 34 weeks.
- HEO-14: card correct, and the DocT expectant-care key is outdated. The card-layer cross line misattributes "34+0–35+6 with recurrent bleeding" to ACOG/SMFM (that is their accreta timing; RCOG gives 34+0–36+6 for praevia with bleeding).
- HEO-15: card correct. Two keys are disputed: carboprost where ergometrine is equally valid, and misoprostol where methylergonovine is safe in a normotensive asthmatic.
- HEO-17: c "Anti-D 50 µg" for a 7-week ectopic is the traditional and exam answer but is now contested (NICE NG126: none before 12 weeks; ACOG 2024: may be forgone after abortion or loss before 12 weeks, ectopic not mentioned in its summaries). a: D-dimer is unreliable alone, but ESC 2019 accepts pregnancy-adapted algorithms. d: IDSA 2019 says 4–7 days; ACOG avoids nitrofurantoin in the first trimester only when an alternative exists.
- HEO-07: the card follows ATA 2016. JTA 2016 differs: either thionamide, iodide at the same time as the thionamide, β1-selective blockers rather than propranolol. The card's "why" misuses the term Jod-Basedow (strictly, that is iodine-induced hyperthyroidism).
