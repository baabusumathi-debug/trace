## Settled points (no change needed)
- UDMI 2026: types 1–5 replaced by primary, secondary and procedure-related MI (ACC). Late (>30 d) stent thrombosis, restenosis and graft failure count as primary, "de novo disease" (ACDIS). Type 3 "will not have a designated type" and is classified "by the setting or post-mortem findings" (ACDIS). MINOCA = myocardial *injury* with non-obstructive coronary arteries, a working diagnosis (ACC, SOLACI). Sex-specific troponin thresholds (ACC). Secondary MI = supply/demand imbalance from another acute condition plus obstructive CAD without acute coronary pathology and/or new or presumed-new RWMA or absent viable myocardium (ACDIS). Procedure-related MI = within 30 days of a percutaneous or surgical cardiac intervention, with troponin multiples no longer confirmatory (SOLACI). The writer's text agrees on all of these.
- UDMI 2018 type 4a/5 criteria, the type 5 evidence list being narrower (no ST-T criterion) and the 10–15 min ultrastructural timing: correct as written (Fourth UDMI). The writer's FLAGS on MI-02 and MI-09 stand.
- Chest-pain likelihood ratios in MI-16/17 (right arm about 4.7, both arms 4.1, pleuritic 0.2, sharp/positional/palpation about 0.3; Swap & Nagurney, JAMA 2005): consistent with the standard figures.
- Nitrate relief (top-up): relief equally common with and without CAD in ED cohorts (Henrikson, Ann Intern Med 2003). The text makes no exact-number claim, so it was kept.
- Traps on disputed keys kept, and their truth side checked: DocT "ibuprofen and reassure" (truth: ECG + troponin + aspirin first); Marrow 21-day DAPT after alteplase (truth: no antiplatelet for 24 h; DAPT for minor stroke NIHSS ≤3 or high-risk TIA without lysis); Marrow V1–V3 depression = NSTEMI (truth: exclude posterior STEMI, V7–V9 ≥0.5 mm).

## Changes
- MI-13 a: the rationale "the 48 h window of 2018 missed early stent thrombosis" was wrong; in 2018 only 4a and 5 had a 48 h window, and 4b/4c had none → now says that, and that 2026 puts every procedural complication in the first 30 days into one category (Fourth UDMI; ACDIS). [factual]
- MI-13 c: "evidence is anatomical or functional" read as complication *or* imaging → now says a procedural coronary complication is the core requirement, with new RWMA or loss of viable myocardium also needed in some settings (SOLACI). [factual]
- MI-13 d: the unsourced claim "multiples … did not track outcome consistently" and the critics' point stated as fact → now gives the mechanism (troponin is released by every procedure, so a multiple is an arbitrary bar not matched across assays), and the critics' point is attributed to the card. [factual, softened]
- MI-14 b: "Most MINOCA patients turn out to have myocarditis, Takotsubo or a non-cardiac cause" → "many … myocarditis, Takotsubo, another cardiomyopathy or PE" (ACDIS; the proportions vary by cohort). [factual]
- MI-15 a: WHO "about 16%" → "about 13–16% of all deaths at all ages (2019–2021)", and the card's "over 35, ~20%" now labelled as not traceable to a primary source (WHO fact sheets). [factual]
- MI-19 e: "non-sustained VT" was given without saying that standard texts name isolated ventricular premature beats as the commonest post-MI rhythm disturbance → mechanism added (border-zone automaticity, triggered activity, re-entry, electrolytes), plus VPBs, AF and VF placed beside it, and a new trap ("commonest arrhythmia after MI is VF") with an exam-day rule. [factual]
- MI-08 c: added the 2026 rule that, without biomarkers or imaging, MI may be presumed from an ischaemic presentation with new ischaemic ECG changes or Q waves (SOLACI). [minor]
- MI-10 intro: an unverifiable exam-date claim ("exam December 2026 will use the old types") → "banks written before August 2026 still key the 2018 types; answer numbered questions with the 2018 number". [minor]
- MI-05 e: "most of what was UA is now NSTEMI, UA becoming rare" → "much … less common (ESC 2023)". [minor]
- T_3a5116ba b: added the ESC 2023 single-sample rule-out condition (very low hs-cTn and pain onset >3 h earlier), because the card's "one negative troponin excludes MI" is too strong. [factual]
- Header comment of cvs10a_learn.py: a second-review line now lists what was re-checked and what stays unverified.

## Final FLAGS
- MI-02 b: the card says 15–20 min; the Fourth UDMI gives "as early as 10–15 min" for the first ultrastructural changes.
- MI-05 b: "usually without significant CAD" understates type 2. The 2018 definition includes fixed CAD with an imbalance, and 2026 makes CAD a criterion. This is soft, not wrong as a contrast.
- MI-09 e: the card applies one evidence list to both types. Under UDMI 2018, type 5 excludes new ischaemic ST-T change and the flow-limiting complication list; it accepts only Q waves, new graft or native occlusion, or imaging.
- MI-13 c (new): "coronary complication and/or imaging" may overstate the "or". Summaries say a complication is required, with imaging added in some scenarios. The full text was not opened.
- MI-15 a (new): "above 35, ~20% of deaths" could not be traced to a primary source; WHO gives 13–16% of all deaths at all ages.
- MI-19 c: free wall rupture presents as tamponade or PEA, not a murmur.
- MI-19 e (new): textbooks give isolated VPBs as the commonest post-MI arrhythmia; NSVT is the notes' answer.
- T_3a5116ba b (new, top-up FLAGS): a single negative troponin excludes MI only if very low and the pain began more than 3 h earlier (ESC 2023).
