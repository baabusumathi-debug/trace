# RESP05 (pulmonary artery catheter): second review, 11 Nov 2026

18 changes: 9 factual corrections or softenings (F), 9 minor additions or clarifications (m). Card text unchanged. `check_learn.py RESP05` prints OK.

## Changes

1. Header (m): the "from standard teaching, not re-checked" line now lists what this review re-checked online, and the sources used.
2. SWAN-03c (F): "No randomised trial has yet reported" → now names the trial: PACCS (NCT05485376; early PAC vs delayed or no PAC; primary outcome in-hospital mortality), listed as recruiting at its last registry update. Also adds the Garan OR's 95% CI (1.06–2.33) and states it is an association only. Sources: CenterWatch/ClinicalTrials.gov listing; Garan JACC HF 2020 abstract.
3. SWAN-04a (F): RVAD reason changed. It said the device "makes the right-heart path and its readings unreliable"; it now says the catheter can tangle with, or be drawn into, cannulae in the right heart, and the device carries the flow being measured. Endocarditis and mechanical valve are now attributed to the UCSF Hospital Handbook, which lists them as absolute contraindications. Source: UCSF Hospital Handbook, Deranged Physiology.
4. SWAN-04c (m): adds that transient catheter-induced RBBB occurs in about 0.05–5% of insertions, and that in a series of 82 LBBB patients none developed complete heart block during insertion (risk real but small). Source: TTUHSC PAC complications chapter.
5. SWAN-06c (F): "the most reliable way to tell RV from PA" (the diastolic slope) → now "a useful extra sign; the diastolic step-up and the notch are the main ones". Source: LHSC Critical Care (diastolic drop with unchanged systolic, the notch, and symmetric vs asymmetric contour are the cues used).
6. SWAN-07a (F): "Most references: PA 15–30/4–12" → Edwards gives RA 2–6, RV 15–25/0–8, PA 15–25/8–15 and wedge 6–12, while some texts give PA diastolic as low as 4. Source: Edwards normal haemodynamic parameters card.
7. SWAN-09d (F): SVI 35–45 → 33–47 mL/m²/beat. Source: Edwards normal values card.
8. SWAN-10b (m): extraction "about 25%" → 22–30%. DO₂I 500–600 and VO₂I 120–160 were confirmed. Source: Edwards.
9. SWAN-11a (F): "5 mL, which is less accurate" → 5 mL also works, but the smaller dip lets rewarming and technique errors weigh more (Elkayam found no significant 5 vs 10 mL difference). Repeats changed from "three times" to "three to five, usually at end-expiration, output varies up to ~10% with breathing". Sources: Deranged Physiology, LITFL.
10. SWAN-11c (F): error directions added. Anything that shrinks the area overestimates output: too little or too warm injectate overestimates, too much underestimates. TR usually underestimates. Shunts make the reading unreliable because the catheter measures right-sided flow. Sources: LITFL, Deranged Physiology.
11. SWAN-12a (m): PAC-Man complication breakdown added (haematoma 4%, arterial puncture 3%, arrhythmias needing treatment 3%). Source: The Bottom Line summary of PAC-Man.
12. SWAN-12b (m): rates added. Sustained VT is unusual and VF rare (1.3–1.5% needed treatment in two series), transient RBBB 0.05–5%, and knotting ~1% is attributed to one review. Sources: TTUHSC chapter, Deranged Physiology.
13. SWAN-12c (m): pulmonary infarction 0–1.4% in one large series, often radiographic only. Source: TTUHSC chapter.
14. SWAN-12d (F): rupture figures. The old text said "one review 0.031–0.05% with 50–70%", which could not be verified. It now says: Kearney and Shabot series, 32,442 patients, 0.031%, 7 of 10 died (70%); other large series 0.06–0.2%; Deranged Physiology 0.2% with 30%. Risk factors now add female sex and the reason for hypothermia (a stiffer catheter). Management restructured: do not pull the catheter out; consider slight withdrawal and re-inflation to occlude the branch; isolate the lung; bleeding side down until isolated, plus PEEP; reverse anticoagulation; embolisation or surgery for definitive control; pseudoaneurysm can rebleed. Sources: PMC3226129 review (Kearney & Shabot), Deranged Physiology rupture chapter, Frontiers Cardiovasc Med 2025 case review.
15. SWAN-12 table and decode (F): "mortality about half or more" → "roughly 30–70%" (decode notes the card's 41–70%).
16. SWAN-13e (m): ESCAPE days alive out of hospital (~133 vs 135) and 6-month mortality (20% vs 17%) added. Source: Wiki Journal Club ESCAPE.
17. SWAN-14 extra (m): Schwann marked observational, with the result stated as in propensity-matched pairs (OR 2.08, 95% CI 1.11–3.88). Source: Anesth Analg 2011 abstract (QxMD).
18. SWAN-14 extra (m): Cochrane RR added (1.02 general ICU, 0.98 high-risk surgery). Source: Rajaram 2013 abstract (QxMD).

## Final FLAGS
- **SWAN-03:** not an error. Garan registry evidence is given, and PACCS (NCT05485376) is now named: recruiting, target 400 (adaptive up to 800), primary outcome in-hospital mortality.
- **SWAN-04:** the card omits right-sided endocarditis and puts mechanical right-heart valves under high risk, where UCSF lists both as absolute. Now verified; the "from memory" wording was removed.
- **SWAN-07:** card ranges differ from references for RA, PA systolic and wedge. Corrected: the card's PA diastolic 8–15 matches Edwards, so it is no longer called narrow.
- **SWAN-09:** LV and RV coronary perfusion pressure are not on the card (unchanged).
- **SWAN-12 (new, minor):** card rupture mortality 41–70%; sources give 30–70%, and a 41% lower bound was not found.
- **SWAN-14:** Chiang is understated as "no benefit" (unchanged).
