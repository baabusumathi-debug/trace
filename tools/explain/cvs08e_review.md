# CVS08E review (independent fact-check, 19 Nov 2026)

Files checked: cvs08e_learn.py (15 cards; no Top-ups for CVS08E). check_learn.py CVS08E: OK (15/15, spine 6). Letters and bold restatements match the cards except one (ACLS-85, fixed).

## Changes

- ACLS-71 mimic: child rule given without its conditions → now: lone rescuer without a phone gives about 2 min CPR first (AHA; ERC 1 min); with a phone, call on speaker and start at once; a child's witnessed sudden collapse is call-and-AED first (AHA 2025 paediatric BLS as recorded in the writer's check; ERC 2021 PBLS). [factual, minor]
- ACLS-72 a: "a heart in VF is rarely in an organised rhythm straight after a successful shock" (wrong framing) → after a successful shock the stunned heart gives little or no output for about a minute (often brief PEA/asystole), so a pulse check finds nothing. [factual]
- ACLS-72 mimic: stacked shocks now limited to "up to three, monitored witnessed VF in the cath lab or after cardiac surgery (ERC)". [minor]
- ACLS-73 a: "pre-shock pauses beyond about 20 s" (vague) → Cheskes, Circulation 2011 (815 shockable OHCA): pre-shock ≥20 s vs <10 s OR 0.47; each 5 s about 18% lower survival; perishock ≥40 s vs <20 s OR 0.54 (PubMed 21690495). [factual precision]
- ACLS-73 b: added that the post-shock pause alone was not independently associated with survival in Cheskes; the recommendation rests on the total perishock pause and physiology (same source). [factual]
- ACLS-73 trap 1: perishock cut-off and OR added (same source). [minor]
- ACLS-74 b: ">6 cm … lower survival" (unsupported) → "more injuries with no added survival". [factual]
- ACLS-76 a: "sniffing position opens the large-occiput airway" (vague) → the large occiput flexes the neck on a flat surface; neutral/slight extension corrects it. [minor, depth]
- ACLS-81 VV b: bare restatement → why of dual-lumen single-site cannulation (no artery needed, less recirculation, mobilisation). [minor, depth]
- ACLS-82 b: "ELSO and the trials use age under about 70 and arrest-to-CPR under 5 min" → ELSO 2021 interim: age <70, witnessed, shockable, no-flow <5 min, low-flow <60 min, consensus-based (as cited in JECT 2025 ECPR review). [factual precision]
- ACLS-82 c: "INCEPTION (134 patients, randomised in transport)" → 160 randomised, 134 analysed, 10 Dutch centres, 20% vs 16% at 30 days, NS (The Bottom Line summary; OR 1.4, p 0.52). ARREST confirmed: 30 randomised (15 vs 15), 43% vs 7%, stopped early for benefit (ACC trial summary). [factual]
- ACLS-82 trap: Surugadai exclusions now attributed "as DocT quotes them" — original protocol could not be retrieved. [softened]
- ACLS-84 a: "the right radial artery is the first branch from the arch" (anatomically wrong) → right subclavian arises from the brachiocephalic trunk, the first branch of the arch. [factual]
- ACLS-84 b: LV filling under VA ECMO attributed only to bronchial/Thebesian flow and AR → main source added: incomplete venous drainage, so some blood still crosses the lungs. [factual]
- ACLS-84 trap 1: "moves the mixing zone up" → "closer to the aortic root". [minor]
- ACLS-85 a: fidelity — bold said "full cardiopulmonary bypass"; card says "full cardiopulmonary support" → matched to card. [fidelity]
- ACLS-85 b: device flows made explicit: IABP 0.5–1 L/min; Impella 2.5 ≈2.5, CP ≈3.5–4, 5.0/5.5 ≈5–5.5; VA ECMO 4–6+ (standard figures). [minor]
- Header: reviewer's sources, dates and the not-re-verified list added.
