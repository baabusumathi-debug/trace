# CVS06A review — independent fact-check (19 Nov 2026)

Files checked: cvs06a_learn.py (22 cards) and cvs06a_topups_learn.py (T_a1c52257). `check_learn.py CVS06A`: OK (22/22, spine 6 steps OK).
Sources: ESC/ERS 2022 PH guideline full text (Humbert, EHJ 2022, ehac237); AJEM review of acute RHF epidemiology; Lung 2025 review (Springer s00408-025-00862-y); Mekontso Dessap ICM 2016 abstract; MedlinePlus "keratoderma with woolly hair"; FPnotebook air embolism.

## Changes
- RVF-02 a (factual): the claim that AHEAD's isolated RHF group had "a mortality comparable to cardiogenic shock subgroups" was invented and wrong → removed. The text now says the 3–9% range comes from pooled European registries (OFICA, ESC-HF pilot, RO-AHFS, EHFS II), 2.2% from CHARITEM and 5–17% mortality from EHFS II (AJEM review; Lung 2025). Mimic reworded to match.
- RVF-01 a and decode (minor): "3–9% from AHEAD" → "3–9%, which the card attributes to AHEAD" (couldn't confirm AHEAD as the source).
- RVF-04 trap (factual/logic): "at a sixth of the pressure it does roughly a quarter of the work" contradicted itself → stroke work = SV × developed pressure; about a quarter of the LV's (sources give a quarter to a sixth).
- RVF-12 c (minor): unclear sentence about LV volume tolerance → the RV's compliance absorbs volume, the LV's thickness protects it against pressure.
- RVF-17 trap (rule): the distractors "Brugada or Marfan" were invented (Marrow options aren't available) → the real confusion, Naxos (JUP, RV) vs Carvajal (DSP, LV-dominant DCM) (MedlinePlus).
- RVF-18 trap (depth): added why LV dysfunction is the wrong answer (PEEP lowers LV preload and afterload). Punch grammar fixed.
- RVF-19 trap (accuracy): Durant's mechanism is now called postulated, with little outcome evidence (FPnotebook).
- RVF-20 trap (rule): the invented "PAH from lupus" distractor → "a raised mPAP with a normal wedge means group 1": a normal wedge only makes PH pre-capillary.
- RVF-20 extra, RVF-22 trap (precision): bosentan in Eisenmenger = ESC/ERS 2022 class I, level B, for symptomatic patients, to improve exercise capacity (Rec. Table 19).
- RVF-21 a (accuracy): "PVR >5 WU = severe, out of proportion" → ESC/ERS 2022 defines severe PH in lung disease as PVR >5 WU, replacing the older pressure-based definition; refer to a PH centre (confirmed in the full text). Mimic reworded.
- RVF-21 c (factual, missing): explanation now states that CHD is no longer in group 5 in 2022 (in 2018 "complex CHD" was 5.4). ESC 2022 has it in 1.4.4 (PAH-CHD, including Eisenmenger) and 2.3 (post-capillary congenital/acquired). It also adds that vasculitis and thyroid disorders have left group 5 (Table 6).
- RVF-22 a/c (fidelity): TOF late pulmonary regurgitation moved from part a (volume categories) to part c (pressure causes), where the card puts post-TOF.
- Header: reviewer sources added; the Harjola/AHEAD figures are now marked as only partly confirmed.

## Verified as correct (no change)
- ESC 2022 definitions (mPAP >20, PAWP ≤15, PVR >2 WU; IpcPH/CpcPH; exercise slope >3); group 1–4 wording; methamphetamine/dasatinib; CTEPH 3.2% of survivors; PEA → BPA → riociguat.
- Mekontso Dessap 2016: 752 patients, ACP 22%; risk factors pneumonia, DP ≥18, P/F <150, PaCO₂ ≥48; overall mortality not different; severe ACP 57% vs 42%.
- Naxos = JUP (plakoglobin), Carvajal = DSP (desmoplakin), mostly recessive; PKP2 is the commonest ARVC gene.
- ASE 2015 cut-offs (3D RVEF <45%, wall >5 mm). Top-up T_a1c52257 (Laplace σ = P·r/2h, determinants of MVO₂, haemoglobin is supply): correct, no changes.
- Traps on disputed keys kept: RVF-20 (DocT mPAP >25), RVF-21 (Marrow IV epoprostenol in stable CTEPH). Truth side checked against ESC 2022.

## Final FLAGS
- RVF-20: "MAP >25" is outdated (ESC/ERS 2022 mPAP >20, PAWP ≤15, PVR >2 WU); the group 1/2 lists use pre-2022 wording (chronic haemolytic anaemia in group 5 since 2013; responders/non-responders, PVOD/PCH renamed; group 2 by HF EF).
- RVF-21: group 4 is now "PH associated with pulmonary artery obstructions"; splenectomy, vasculitis, thyroid disorders and congenital heart disease are no longer group 5 (CHD → groups 1 and 2); 2022 group 5 also lists PTTM and fibrosing mediastinitis. Marrow's IV epoprostenol key for stable CTEPH conflicts with ESC 2022.
- RVF-02 (new): the 3–9% / 5–17% figures come from pooled registries; attributing them to AHEAD alone couldn't be confirmed.
- RVF-22: post-TOF is mainly volume (PR) late, with pressure from residual obstruction.
- RVF-17: TGA under volume overload is questionable (a systemic RV is pressure-loaded).
- RVF-07: RVEF ≥40% / wall ≤3 mm are MRI-type ranges; ASE 2015 echo: RVEF <45% abnormal, wall >5 mm = RVH.
- RVF-18: "survival much higher without RV dysfunction" overstates the data; only severe ACP raised mortality (57% vs 42%).
- RVF-05: the 80/20 RCA/left split is coronary dominance; the LAD also supplies the anterior RV and the moderator band.
