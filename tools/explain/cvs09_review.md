## Writer's open points, settled
- ACC/AHA 2022 targets: SBP <120 mmHg or the lowest BP that keeps end-organ perfusion, HR 60–80 b.p.m., **class 1, C-LD**. IV beta-blocker first (1, B-NR); IV non-DHP CCB if contraindicated (2a, B-NR); pain control (1, C-EO). Source: ACC/AHA 2022 AAS recommendation table (PMC9860464, table T88) and the ACC "Key Perspectives" summary. "Within the first hour" is not in the 2022 recommendation, so it is now given as common protocol practice.
- IRAD 2013 time classes (Booher, Am J Med 2013): hyperacute 0–24 h, acute 2–7 days, subacute 8–30 days, chronic >30 days. Confirmed (abstract). ESC 2024: <24 h, 1–14 d, 15–90 d, >90 d. Confirmed (OUP full text).
- TEM codes (Sievers 2020): E0–E3, M0–M3 with +/−. The text matches the standard description. ESC 2024 text confirms Type/Entry/Malperfusion and the non-A non-B definition.
- Hypothermia grades (Yan 2013 consensus): profound ≤14, deep 14.1–20, moderate 20.1–28, mild 28.1–34 °C. The text is correct.
- Drug-label doses: esmolol, nicardipine, diltiazem, nitroprusside (0.3 to 10 mcg/kg/min, ≤10 min at max, cyanide above about 2 mcg/kg/min, thiocyanate 60 mg/L) and nitroglycerin match the labels. Labetalol repeat bolus: the label gives 40–80 mg, not the card's 20–80 mg (FLAG added). Clevidipine does not appear in the deck.
- Left renal artery from the false lumen: confirmed (Radiopaedia, "usually arises from the false lumen"; coeliac, SMA and right renal usually from the true lumen).
- CSF drain: CSF pressure about 10 mmHg (8–12), at most about 15–20 mL/h, lower if a deficit appears, MAP raised, Hb >10. The card's <10 / <5 mmHg and 15 mL/h sit within published protocols. I could not open a second primary protocol (the fetch needed approval), so this rests on the writer's Endovascular Today 2020 source plus standard practice.
- ASRA 2018 holds (top-up): confirmed as the writer gave them.
