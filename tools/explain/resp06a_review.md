## Changes (14: 6 factual, 8 minor)

1. **PE-03a, factual:** the text only gave the card's "peak in the first 2 weeks". It now adds that in the Million Women Study, risk of admission with VTE was highest in the first 6 weeks, **peaked in week 3** and was still raised at 7–12 weeks. FLAG added. Source: Sweetland et al., BMJ 2009;339:b4583.
2. **PE-03c, factual:** "cumulative figures from population cohorts" (no source named) → Heit 2000 Olmsted County: 12.9% at 1 y, 22.8% at 5 y, 30.4% at 10 y. These include possible recurrences. The same cohort gives 8.3% at 90 d and 10.1% at 180 d, so the card's "8% within 6 months" does not match; FLAG added. Source: Heit, Arch Intern Med 2000 (JAMA Network abstract and results).
3. **PE-11d, factual:** "planar V/Q is non-diagnostic in about half" → "diagnostic in only about 30–50% of emergency patients". SPECT non-diagnostic <3% is kept. Source: ESC 2019 text; ESC summary (angiology.org).
4. **PE-06a, factual (unverifiable figure softened):** "These frequencies come from a large emergency-department registry" → frequencies vary by series; PIOPED reported dyspnoea in 73% and pleuritic pain in 66%; the order matters more than the number. I could not confirm the card's 50/39/23/24/8/6% against EMPEROR (Pollack, JACC 2011) online, because the full text was not reachable. Source for PIOPED: Medscape 300901.
5. **PE-07a, factual (softened):** "Older data (PIOPED) found non-specific ST–T changes as common as or commoner than tachycardia" → "Older series often reported…". I could not verify the PIOPED I figure online.
6. **PE-03b, factual (completed):** the PH definition now gives the full 2022 ESC/ERS criteria: mPAP >20, PAWP ≤15, **PVR >2 WU**, after ≥3 months of anticoagulation.
7. **PE-01 intro, minor:** VITAE named properly: Cohen 2007, six EU countries, ~370,000 VTE deaths; 34% sudden fatal, 59% undiagnosed, 7% diagnosed antemortem. Source: VITAE abstract (Thromb Haemost 2007;98:756) and ESC 2019 text.
8. **PE-01 trap, minor:** autopsy figure made exact: 2.0% (14 of 696 autopsied adult inpatient deaths, 2007–08), about 1% of all inpatient deaths. "Teaching-hospital" removed. Source: JRSM 2011;104:327.
9. **PE-12a, minor:** "later studies found it insensitive (19%)" now attributed to Kurzyna 2002 (100 suspected PE: McConnell sensitivity 19%, specificity 100%). "About two-thirds of RV infarctions" now attributed to Casazza 2005. Sources: thebottomline summary of Kurzyna 2002; Casazza 2005 (sensitivity 70%, specificity 33%).
10. **PE-12a, minor:** 60/60 figures (94% specific, 25% sensitive) attributed to the Kurzyna cohort. Same source.
11. **PE-08d, minor (depth):** the simplified Wells part had no explanation. It now says why the rule exists and how it is used (two-level only).
12. **Top-up c, minor (softened):** "most abdominal or pelvic cancer surgery falls in the high group" → it "usually reaches" high, because cancer and major surgery each add 2 Caprini points. Caprini bands (0 / 1–2 / 3–4 / ≥5) are confirmed (Gould, Chest 2012, via MSD Manual table).
13. **Top-up table, minor:** title "Surgical risk tiers (ACCP)" → "ACCP 2008, with the card's examples". Maxillofacial surgery and splenectomy are not ACCP 2008 examples. ACCP 2008 tiers confirmed: low <10%, moderate 10–40%, high 40–80%, with the high-tier examples (Geerts 2008 table, PMC2685237).
14. **File headers (both files):** the "from memory" lines were replaced with what this review verified and what it could not find.

## Verified and left as written

- **ESC 2019 text** (OUP full text):
  - 10/30/65% PE prevalence by probability class
  - SaO₂ normal in ≤40%, A–a gradient normal in 20%
  - sinus tachycardia 40%
  - non-preconditioned RV cannot exceed a mean PAP of 40 mmHg
  - echo NPV 40–50%, RV dilatation in ≥25%
  - CUS shows DVT in 30–50%
  - late recurrence 2.5%/yr (transient factor) vs 4.5%/yr (no factor)
  - CTEPH 0.1–9.1% at 2 y
  - the terms "provoked/unprovoked" avoided
  - point-of-care D-dimer 88% sensitivity

  - Cook 2005: four factors, HR 4.0 / 3.7 / 3.2 / 2.8
