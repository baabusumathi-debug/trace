### Minor (precision and sourcing)
- MI-30 cMyC trap: added the evidence. APACE (Kaier, Circulation 2017): AUC about 0.92, the same as hs-cTnT/I; rule-out at presentation 32% vs 25% with hs-cTnT; better in patients presenting within 3 h. Copeptin: BIC-8 (Möckel 2015), negative copeptin plus negative troponin allowed safe early discharge; most useful with conventional troponin. ESC 2023: only CK-MB, cMyC and copeptin "may have clinical relevance", with limited value beyond hs-cTn.
- MI-34 d: "CMR is the key test in MINOCA" → adds ESC 2023 I, B (CMR when the cause is unclear after angiography). Source: ESC 2023 recommendation, as tabulated in J Clin Med 2024 (PMC11012418).
- MI-29 d: the 6%/3% cytoplasmic pools are confirmed as the classic figures (Babuin & Jaffe, CMAJ 2005). The header had said "6–8% / 3–4%" and is now corrected. A note was added that later work suggests a larger early-releasable pool (J Clin Med 2025 review).
- MI-20 b: the extension of defibrillator-capable monitoring to other suspected ACS now carries its class (I, B), confirmed in the ESC 2023 Recommendation Table 1.
- MI-23 d: added that AHA/ACC 2025 also says new LBBB in isolation is not diagnostic of MI.

- Top-up part a (Wellens): "many go on to a large anterior MI" → about three-quarters of medically treated patients in the original de Zwaan series had an anterior MI within weeks. Added that ESC 2023 names Wellens’ sign. Source: StatPearls, Wellens Syndrome.

## Final FLAGS
- **MI-22** (incomplete, not wrong): 1 mm in V4R is the classic cut. The Universal Definition accepts ≥0.5 mm in V3R/V4R (≥1 mm in men under 30), and in V7–V9 0.5 mm (1 mm in men under 40).
- **MI-27:** Takotsubo is listed only under ST depression, but it commonly gives ST elevation. LVH appears twice in the ST depression column.
- **MI-29:** rows a and c are reversed. TnT binds tropomyosin and TnI inhibits actomyosin ATPase. cTnT (one manufacturer) has a single reference level; cTnI has several assay-dependent ones.
- **MI-30:** CK-MB "for re-infarction" is outdated. Use troponin (>20% rise from a stable or falling raised value); CK-MB only if troponin is unavailable.
- **MI-31 (new):** "performs better in CKD" holds only against conventional assays. In CKD, hs-cTn specificity and the share ruled out are lower than in normal renal function. The wording is ambiguous, not wrong.
