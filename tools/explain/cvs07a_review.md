- PCA-19 a (factual): said the card's "33% seizures / myoclonus more common 20–25%" is a contradiction with no traced source → now says the pair comes from ERC 2015/2021: about one-third of comatose survivors have seizures, and myoclonus is the commonest type (18–25%), so it is a subset. ILCOR 2008's 10–40% is kept. (ERC 2015/2021 post-resuscitation care; ILCOR 2008 PCAS statement)

## Verified, no change
- Goto 2016 (JAHA, n 17,238, full text): overall 36.8%/21.8%. After 20 min, 4.6%/1.9%; after 30 min, 0.8%/0.4%; no survivors beyond 53 min; over 99% of CPC 1–2 by 35 min (42 min for asystole). The writer's PCA-01 FLAG is right.
- BOX (MAP 63 vs 77; PaO₂ 68–75 vs 98–105, no difference), COACT (552; 90-day survival 64.5% vs 67.2%), TOMAHAWK (554; 30-day death 54.0% vs 46.0%) and TELSTAR (172; 90% vs 92%) match the trial reports as I know them. The text quotes no figures that go beyond these.
- Pittsburgh category (top-up): I awake; II coma with SOFA CV+resp <4; III coma with SOFA CV+resp ≥4; IV coma with lost brainstem reflexes. Survival about 80/60/40/10% (Rittenberger 2011; Coppler 2015 validation, n 607). No change to the top-up file.
- ILCOR 2008 phases, PCAS components and mechanisms; AHA 2025 classes for angiography, temperature control (32–37.5 °C, ≥36 h), oxygen (90–98%), PaCO₂, MAP ≥65 and myoclonus without an EEG correlate: consistent.
- DocT GT8 MAP >80 key: the trap says it is wrong and gives the exam-day answer, which agrees with the cross line.

## Not verified
- AHA 2025 chain-of-survival link names: the primary Part 3 text was not opened and secondary sources only were used (writer's). The PCA-04 text keeps them as "essentially the same six links".
- ERC 2015/2021 seizure and myoclonus figures: from standard reference; the text was not reopened (search budget exhausted).

## Final FLAGS
- PCA-01: card figures do not match Goto 2016. At 20 min, CPC 1–2 is 1.9% (card 0.8%). At 30 min, survival is 0.8% (card 0.4%) and CPC 1–2 is 0.4% (card 0%). The 1-minute row is the cohort's overall rate, and the watch line is wrong.
- PCA-04: card gives the AHA 2020 adult OHCA chain; AHA 2025 uses a single chain for all settings with essentially the same six links.
- PCA-10: the dose box is from the older (2010/2015) AHA algorithm; the AHA 2025 algorithm has no dose panel and targets PaCO₂ 35–45 and MAP ≥65.
- PCA-17: "noradrenaline first (ERC–ESICM 2025)" overstates the guideline, which says fluids, noradrenaline and/or dobutamine according to need. "SBP >90" differs from ERC 2025's "SBP >100 or MAP >60–65". The DocT MAP >80 key is not supported by guidelines.
- PCA-19: "myoclonus is more common: 20–25%" is misleading wording. Myoclonus is the commonest type (18–25%) within the roughly 33% who have seizures (ERC 2015/2021). The card's watch line calling it a contradiction is itself wrong.
