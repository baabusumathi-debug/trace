## Verified, no change needed
- Repeat troponin. ESC 2023: 0 h/1 h is best, 0 h/2 h second-best, and 0 h/3 h is an alternative only where those are unavailable (ESC text). AHA/ACC 2021 chest pain guideline: hs-cTn repeat at 1–3 h, conventional assay at 3–6 h (memory). MI-75 is correct.
- P2Y12 monotherapy timing. ACC/AHA 2025: ticagrelor monotherapy ≥1 month after PCI is Class 1 (Cleveland Clinic summary). ESC 2023: 3–6 months (IIa) as above. MI-72 is correct.
- IVUS/OCT Class 1 (1, A) in ACC/AHA 2025: confirmed by HealthManagement and the ACC journal scan. Complete revascularisation Class 1 outside shock: confirmed by Cleveland Clinic. Microaxial pump 2a, and routine IABP and VA-ECMO class 3: confirmed by ACC Year in Review and NYU.
- DanGer Shock: 355 patients; 180-day death 45.8% vs 58.5% (absolute difference 12.7%) (ACC Year in Review).
- Retroperitoneal bleed risk factors: high puncture, female sex, low body weight/BSA, GP IIb/IIIa or heavy anticoagulation, large sheaths. "Obesity" on the card is a weaker, indirect factor (it makes a high puncture more likely). The writer's flag is kept.
- UDMI 2026, REDUCE-AMI, pooled 40–49% analysis, ABYSS, lipid and rehab classes: consistent with the writer's sources and with my knowledge; no change.
