# RESP03 review: independent fact-check (10 Nov)

## Changes
- RSC-02 b (factual): "needed more sedation in some patients" → the use and duration of sedatives and neuromuscular blockers did not differ; day-1 PaCO₂ was higher in the low arm; protocol details added (start at 6, down 1 mL/kg/h to 4; intermediate arm starts at 10). Source: Simonis et al., JAMA 2018 (summary at thebottomline.org.uk/summaries/PreVent).
- RSC-02 a (minor): "expected to stay ventilated beyond the next day" → "expected not to be extubated within 24 h; Berlin ARDS an exclusion". Source: as above.
- RSC-02 c (addition): median 21 VFDs in both arms; no difference in severe atelectasis or pneumothorax; Surviving Sepsis 2026 suggests 6–8 mL/kg over 4 to <6 mL/kg without ARDS (conditional, low certainty). Sources: as above; SSC 2026 adult guideline (sccm.org).
- RSC-13 b (factual): RR 0.33 and 0.43 given as if both were benefits → the meta-analysis (11 studies, 768 patients, BMI ≥35) found respiratory complications RR 0.33 (95% CI 0.16–0.66, significant), but unplanned ICU admission RR 0.43 (95% CI 0.16–1.15) and re-intubation were not significant. Source: "Perioperative noninvasive ventilation in obese patients: a qualitative review and meta-analysis", 2016 (PubMed 26948450, abstract via QxMD).
- RSC-13 a (factual nuance): "Start CPAP 2 weeks before surgery" left as the card's statement, with the note that CPOC aims for 4–6 weeks, so 2 weeks is a minimum rather than the ideal. Source: CPOC perioperative OSA guidance (2021; printable PDF 2023).
- RSC-03 a (minor): "ARDSNet allows 4–8 mL/kg, starting at 6" → ARDSNet targets 6 mL/kg within a range of 4–8 (the protocol starts at 8 and comes down to 6). Source: ARDSNet protocol card.
- RSC-03 c (factual): "The guideline still suggests traditional recruitment" (no year given) → "Surviving Sepsis 2021 still suggested", because the 2026 recruitment wording could not be confirmed. ART figures added: 28-day mortality 55.3% vs 49.3%, barotrauma 5.6% vs 1.6%, PEEP steps 25/35/45; the recommendation against an incremental-PEEP strategy is strong. Sources: ART summary (thebottomline.org.uk/summaries/ART); SSC 2026 (strong, moderate certainty against incremental PEEP).
- RSC-03 d (minor): PROSEVA inclusion made complete (P/F <150 on FiO₂ ≥0.6, PEEP ≥5, after stabilisation), with 28-day mortality 16% vs 33%. Source: Guérin, NEJM 2013 (standard reference).
- RSC-01 a (addition): 2026 oxygen suggestion described as conditional and low certainty; most trials compared about 90–93% with ≥96%; the panel aimed for 90–96%. Source: SSC 2026 adult guideline (sccm.org).
- RSC-01 b (addition): FLORALI excluded PaCO₂ >45; 90-day mortality 12% vs 23% vs 28%; intubation 38% vs 47% vs 50% (P = 0.18); SSC 2026 keeps high flow as the initial therapy over NIV (conditional, low certainty). Sources: Frat, NEJM 2015 (WikiJournalClub); SSC 2026.
- RSC-01 c (addition): ICU-ROX sepsis figures: 251 patients, 36.2% vs 29.2% 90-day mortality, P = 0.24, post hoc and underpowered. Source: Young et al., Intensive Care Med 2020 (Monash research portal abstract).
- RSC-01 trap (minor): FLORALI intubation reduction exists only in the P/F ≤200 subgroup (35% vs 53% vs 58%). Source: WikiJournalClub FLORALI.
- RSC-04 c (factual softening): "A low albumin is the strongest single laboratory predictor" → "albumin <35 g/L is the most useful laboratory marker of risk (ACP 2006)"; high-risk procedures now listed as thoracic, upper abdominal, aortic, neurosurgical, long or emergency surgery. Source: ACP 2006 guideline (summary in Today's Hospitalist).
- RSC-06 b (factual softening): "it reduced post-operative pneumonia and unplanned intubation" → before/after figures from Boston University Medical Center (pneumonia 2.6% → 1.6%, P = 0.09; unplanned intubation 2.0% → 1.2%). Source: Cassidy et al., JAMA Surg 2013 (MDedge summary).
- RSC-07 b (minor): IMPROVE's 10.5% vs 27.5% is now described as a composite of pulmonary and extrapulmonary complications at 7 days, with NIV/intubation 5% vs 17%; PROVHILO 40% vs 39%, 900 patients; "the benefit came mostly from" → "probably came mostly from". IMPROVE's "less atelectasis and pneumonia" (card) is not in the abstract and could not be checked; it is left as the card's statement and not elaborated. Sources: Futier, NEJM 2013 (QxMD abstract); PROVHILO, Lancet 2014 (QxMD abstract).
- RSC-08 a/b (minor): tidal volume not associated (OR 1.05); PEEP not entered into the model; OR about 3 when a PEEP increase raised driving pressure. Source: Neto et al., Lancet Respir Med 2016 (PubMed 26947624).
- RSC-09 a/b (minor): adds that ERS/ATS makes no recommendation for de novo hypoxaemic failure, and certainty levels (mortality moderate, intubation low). RR 0.28 (0.09–0.84) and 0.27 (0.12–0.61) confirmed. Source: Rochwerg et al., ERS/ATS NIV guideline, Eur Respir J 2017 (ATS PDF).
- RSC-10 a (addition): WSACS IAH grades I–IV (12–15, 16–20, 21–25, >25 mmHg). Definitions of IAH ≥12, ACS >20 with new organ dysfunction, normal 5–7, measurement conditions confirmed. Source: WSACS 2013 consensus summary (wsacs.org).
- RSC-10 b/c (nuance): transmission averages about 50%; lung compliance is unchanged in healthy lungs but may fall in already injured lungs. Source: Regli, Pelosi, Malbrain, Ann Intensive Care 2019 (PMC6484068).
- RSC-11 b (softening): the (IAP – 13) ÷ 2 / target – 7 + 0.7 × IAP correction is now labelled a physiological proposal not tested in trials. Source: as above.
- RSC-11 d (addition): EPVent targets stated (end-expiratory transpulmonary 0–10, end-inspiratory <25 cmH₂O). Source: Talmor, NEJM 2008 (Cooper EM Daily summary).
- RSC-14 c (addition): PROBESE details (2013 patients, BMI ≥35, 7 mL/kg PBW, PEEP 12 + recruitment vs 4; PPC 21.3% vs 23.6%; hypotension 31.6% vs 17.2%; more bradycardia; less intra-operative hypoxaemia). Sources: PROBESE, JAMA 2019 (Penn State Pure abstract; thebottomline.org.uk).
- RSC-15 a (softening): "they gain at least as much oxygenation as others" → attributed to the De Jong 2013 case–control study, with mortality benefit not proven. The full paper could not be opened (link.springer.com/article/10.1186/cc12054 and pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3642540 were blocked), so this is kept cautious.

Checked and left unchanged: SSC 2021 "insufficient evidence" on conservative oxygen and HFNC over NIV (weak); 6 mL/kg strong; plateau <30; intermittent NMBA boluses after ROSE; VV-ECMO in experienced centres; ARMA 6 vs 12 mL/kg (about 40% → 31%); Melbourne Group Score items and the 4-of-8 rule (a published table lists WCC >11.2 and the ICU/HDU item, which supports the MGS-2 note); ICOUGH components and frequencies; Mahul 2016 (T-piece or PS 0/PEEP 0 reflects post-extubation effort); ATS 2019 OHS (BMI ≥30, PaCO₂ ≥45 mmHg, HCO₃⁻ <27 to exclude when suspicion is low, CPAP first-line if severe OSA); PBW arithmetic; FiO₂/absorption atelectasis mechanism. check_learn.py RESP03: OK.

## Final FLAGS
- RSC-02: NEW (not an error). SSC 2026 now suggests 6–8 mL/kg IBW over 4 to <6 mL/kg in sepsis without ARDS, in line with PReVENT; consider adding to the card.
- RSC-05: card WCC >11 vs published >11.2 × 10⁹/L; MGS-2 swaps breath sounds for an ICU/HDU respiratory item. (kept)
- RSC-10: NEW. Card c "lung compliance unchanged" holds for healthy lungs; in injured lungs IAH can lower lung compliance too. Teaching simplification.
- RSC-12: card mentions OHS only as "low drive"; Marrow tests its definition and the HCO₃⁻ → ABG screen. Consider adding. (kept)
- RSC-13: failed-RSI question keys airway pre-assessment, which the card does not cover (kept). UPDATED: card b's unplanned-ICU RR 0.43 was not significant (CI 0.16–1.15); card a's "CPAP 2 weeks before surgery" is shorter than CPOC's 4–6 weeks.
