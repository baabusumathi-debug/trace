- AHA 2025 energies and classes: AF ≥200 J (2a), flutter 200 J (2b; 50 J gives 50–75% success).
- Wide-complex tachycardia classes: adenosine 2b; verapamil and diltiazem 3: Harm; amiodarone, procainamide or sotalol 2b.

- Top-ups (VPB, sick sinus syndrome, unipolar vs bipolar leads): accurate, no changes.

## Final FLAGS
- ACLS-22: c: AHA 2025 PALS gives 4 J/kg for subsequent shocks but allows higher (to 10 J/kg or the adult maximum). The watch line "2 → 4 → 4, not 2 → 4 → 6" is the default, not a ban on going higher. The first dose may be 2–4 J/kg.
- ACLS-30 (card layer line, not the card): "procainamide first choice only with preserved LV function (PROCAMIO)" misattributes PROCAMIO, which included structural heart disease (mean EF about 40%) and favoured procainamide there too.
- ACLS-32: c: sotalol 100 mg (1.5 mg/kg) over 5 min is the 2020 algorithm dose. The 2025 tachyarrhythmia algorithm no longer mentions sotalol, though the 2025 text keeps it (2b).
- ACLS-35: "Bradycardia = HR <60/min" is the ECG definition. AHA 2025 uses typically <50/min for bradyarrhythmia.

Could not verify: the ERC 2025 paediatric SVT cardioversion dose (only the 2021 figure, 1 → 2 J/kg up to 4 J/kg, was found); the exact shock number at which ERC 2025 paediatric escalation begins (sources say from the 5th or after 5 shocks).
