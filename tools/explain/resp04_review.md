# RESP04 review: independent fact-check (10 Nov)

## Changes
- RSC-19 a (factual): "in about half of them no organism is found" (unsourced) → removed; 30–60% now described as most reviews, with two-thirds at the high end. Source: Balkan Med J 2017 review of non-infectious HSCT lung complications.
- RSC-19 b (minor, addition): Spitzer criteria made complete. It now says within 96 h of engraftment; 3 major (non-infectious fever, rash, non-cardiogenic pulmonary oedema/hypoxaemia), or 2 major + 1 minor (weight gain >2.5%, hepatic or renal dysfunction, transient encephalopathy). Source: Clinical Hematology International (Atlantis Press) Table 1; Balkan Med J 2017.
- RSC-19 b (addition): DAH incidence 5–12%, with a median onset that varies by series from about day 11 to day 30. Sources: Balkan Med J 2017 (median day 19, range 5–34); Keklik, Haematologica 2018 (5% of 1,228, median day 30, range 3–168).
- RSC-19 c (factual): IPS "median of about three weeks" → "median onset about day 21–65 in different series". Mortality 60–80% and >95% once ventilated confirmed. Source: Balkan Med J 2017.
- RSC-19 c (minor): etanercept trial named (BMT CTN 0403, 34 adults, stopped early for slow accrual; 62.5% etanercept vs 66.7% placebo, P = 1.00). Source: Yanik et al., BBMT 2014 abstract (experts.umn.edu).
- RSC-19 mimic and trap (factual softening): "acute GVHD rarely involves the lung" → acute GVHD targets skin, gut and liver, and acute lung GVHD is not an accepted entity. Source: ASH Education 2007 GVHD review ("not fully accepted").
- RSC-20 b (factual, completeness): Nocardia "6–12 months" → 6–12 months, at least 12 months if immunosuppressed or CNS disease; imipenem or amikacin added for CNS or severe disease. Source: Harrison's (20th ed.) nocardiosis treatment-duration table. Halo sign (early, neutropenic), air crescent (neutrophil recovery) and reversed halo (mucormycosis) confirmed (Radiogyan halo-sign review).
- RSC-21 b (minor, addition): Lemiale arms made explicit: 24.1% NIV vs 27.3% oxygen; intubation 38.2% vs 44.8%; ICU-acquired infection and length of stay no different. "Removing the room" → "leaving far less room". Source: respiratory-therapy.com summary of Lemiale, JAMA 2015.
- RSC-21 c (minor): FLORALI subgroup NIV arm described as NIV alternating with high flow. 82 patients (30/26/26); intubation 43% / 31% / 65% confirmed. Source: Azoulay "Oxygenation" slides, CHU Mondor (data of Frat, Crit Care 2016). HIGH 35.6% vs 36.1% and 38.7% vs 43.8% confirmed (same source). FLORALI-IM 35% (NIV+HFNC) vs 36% (HFNC) confirmed (HealthManagement.org ISICEM 2022 report).
- RSC-21 e (minor): ERS/ATS 2017 wording quoted exactly ("We suggest early NIV for immunocompromised patients with ARF", conditional, moderate certainty). Source: ATS guideline PDF (member.thoracic.org).
- RSC-22 b (factual): "each roughly doubled the odds of death" → adjusted odds ratios about 1.9 (vasopressors), 2.0 (RRT), 1.7 (undetermined cause). Source: EFRAIM ARDS secondary analysis, Chest 2020 (pure.psu.edu abstract). Hospital mortality 56.3%, driving and plateau pressure and compliance linked, tidal volume not, neutropenia and disease type not: confirmed.
- RSC-23 d (softening): Pierson "average 14.6 mL/kg" → "far larger than today's (about 14 mL/kg)". 39 of 1,700 (2%) with a leak >24 h after the drain and 67% mortality confirmed. Sources: Relias Media (Pierson); JTD "Persistent air leak management in critically ill patients". Prolonged air leak after resection beyond 5–7 days confirmed (same JTD review).
- RSC-25 a (factual): "A left-sided double-lumen tube is usually chosen" given as the rule for a fistula → the bronchial limb goes to the healthy lung, away from the fistula. Left is the elective default because of the right upper lobe takeoff, but a left-sided fistula (e.g. left pneumonectomy stump) needs a right-sided tube or a blocker. Source: standard thoracic anaesthesia teaching (DLT into the "good" side, avoiding suture lines; BPF anaesthesia notes).
- RSC-25 mimic (softening): jet "100–200 breaths a minute" now attributed to the card, with a note that devices and reports vary (neonatal jets run at 4–11 Hz). Oscillation 3–15 Hz with active expiration confirmed. Source: Wikipedia "High-frequency ventilation" summary table.
- RSC-26 c (minor): valve series attributed (Travaline 2009, 40 patients, about 93%). Source: JTD persistent-air-leak review.
- RSC-27 a (factual precision): "P/F above 300 mmHg on PEEP 5" → PaO₂ >300 mmHg on FiO₂ 1.0 and PEEP 5 cmH₂O, as the ideal criterion, with no defined lower limit. Source: Trillium Gift of Life lung deceased-donor criteria v6.0 (2024).
- RSC-28 c (minor): Mascia recipient 6-month survival given (69% vs 75%). 118 donors, eligibility 54% → 95%, harvested 27% → 54% confirmed. Source: MDedge summary of Mascia, JAMA 2010. French cohort (1,626 donors; PV = VT ≤8 mL/kg PBW and PEEP ≥8; OR 1.43, 95% CI 1.03–1.98; 1-year survival 82.7% vs 82.3%) confirmed. Source: AJRCCM 2020 abstract (bib-bvb record).

Checked and left unchanged: Hilbert 2001 (12/26 vs 20/26 intubated; ICU death 10 vs 18; hospital death 13 vs 21 = 46/77, 38/69, 50/81%), from the QxMD abstract; Antonelli 2000 (4/20 vs 14/20 intubated; ICU deaths 4 vs 10), from Relias Media; LUNG SAFE immunocompromised (584/2,813, 20.8%; mortality 52.4% vs 36.2%; first-line NIV 20.9% vs 15.9%; 48% of NIV patients intubated, mortality equal to primary intubation), from the Crit Care 2018 abstract (DOAJ); EFRAIM (1,611 patients; bacterial 29.5%, viral 15.4%, fungal 14.7%, undetermined 13.2%; undetermined linked to intubation HR 1.46 and death OR 1.43), from the ICM 2017 full text (core.ac.uk); Aspergillus risk with neutropenia >10 days (EORTC host factor); BOS criteria and median onset about one year; ATS 2011 IPS criteria; DAH progressively bloodier aliquots. Schmidt ECMO (203 patients, 6-month survival 30%): the abstract could not be opened; the figure matches the card and the writer's online check, and is left as is. check_learn.py RESP04: OK.

## Final FLAGS
- RSC-19: Watch gives DAH "median day 11" (and RSC-29c "first 30 days"), the classic teaching. Later series differ: one review gives median day 19 (range 5–34), and a large cohort (Keklik, Haematologica 2018: 5% of 1,228 allogeneic recipients) median day 30 (range 3–168). Card a's "two-thirds" is above the 30–60% most reviews give. Card c lists acute GVHD as an early lung complication, but acute lung GVHD is not an accepted entity. DocT's 4-week rash + lung key ("GVHD of lung and skin") is the least wrong option; engraftment syndrome, not on the card, fits better. Consider adding it to the card. (Edited: "59 cases" and "mostly within 2 weeks of engraftment" removed as unverified; GVHD point added.)
- RSC-21: incomplete, not an error. FLORALI-IM (2022, 299 patients): NIV+HFNC no better than HFNC alone (35% vs 36%). ERS 2022 conditionally prefers HFNC over NIV. Watch's LUNG SAFE point is verified. (Kept.)
- RSC-25 (new): card a gives no side rule for the double-lumen tube. With a fistula the bronchial limb goes to the healthy lung, so a left-sided fistula needs a right-sided tube or a blocker; the Marrow note "left DLT has wider safety margin" is the elective default only. Card c's jet figures could not be confirmed against a single source.
- RSC-26: Watanabe spigots are listed under proximal defects; they are segmental plugs used mostly for peripheral leaks. (Kept.)
- RSC-28: "peak pressure <30"; protocols usually set the limit as a plateau pressure. (Kept.)
