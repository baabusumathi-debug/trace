Controls, sources and limits

Mouse wheel, arrows, slider or single-finger vertical drag scroll the scan. Right-drag changes width and level. Pinch zooms; double-tap resets zoom and pan. HU probe and ruler read simulated voxel values only.

Real CT comparison, ASPECTS, spotter and find-it are planned for later phases. The model’s deformation and density rules are illustrative, not clinical predictions.
